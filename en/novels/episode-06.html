Episode Six: Journey

In search of the second volume of The Tale of Hero Klaus, Arvel and his companions make their first journey to Dorgaf.

Episode Six: Journey

Story

The next morning.

After finishing their usual training in the yard, Arvel and the others sat down to breakfast.

“Arvel.”

Kieval, seated across from him, paused his meal.

“Are you still reading the story of Klaus?”

“Yeah.”

Arvel nodded without hesitation.

He had lost count of how many times he had reread The Tale of Hero Klaus, the book Rokel had given him. Yet he never grew tired of it. Every time he finished it, he found himself even more eager to know what happened next.

“Father, I want the second volume.”

“The continuation?”

“Yeah. Do you think they sell it anywhere?”

“Let's see...”

Kieval looked up thoughtfully.

“There aren't any shops that sell books in Finridge. Why don't you ask Darius?”

“Darius?”

“Yes. He travels outside the village to buy stock. He might know something.”

Arvel's expression brightened at those words.

By the time breakfast was over, his thoughts were already fixed on the general store.

◇

Darius, who ran the general store in Finridge, was also the son of Ruben, the village chief.

His shop carried everything essential to village life, from everyday goods such as pots and farming tools to the weapons and equipment needed by the village watch. Unlike a large town with many specialized shops, Finridge relied on this single store for a wide range of goods.

When Arvel reached the shop, a wagon was parked out front.

A man beside the wagon bed was checking its condition.

“Good morning, Darius.”

“Hm?”

Hearing the voice, the man looked up.

“Oh, Arvel. Morning. What brings you here so early?”

“There's something I want to ask you.”

“What is it?”

“Do you know The Tale of Hero Klaus?”

“Yes, I do.”

“I want the second volume. Do you have it here?”

Arvel asked hopefully, but Darius shook his head apologetically.

“I've got the children's fairy-tale edition that collects the story in one book, but that's all.”

“I see...”

Darius laughed when he saw Arvel's shoulders visibly slump.

“Don't look so disappointed. We may not have it here, but you might find it if you go to Dorgaf.”

“Dorgaf...”

“It's much bigger than Finridge. There are shops that sell books too. Would you like to come with me?”

“What!?”

Arvel looked up.

Even if the book was in Dorgaf, he could not possibly go there alone. That thought must have shown on his face.

“I was just about to head there to restock.”

“Really!?”

His downcast face lit up in an instant.

“Sure. I don't mind. But ask Kieval properly first.”

“I will! I'll go ask him!”

The moment he answered, Arvel took off running toward home.

◇

“Dorgaf?”

After hearing the story from his returning son, Kieval repeated the name without looking particularly surprised.

“Yeah. Darius asked if I wanted to go with him. Can I?”

“If you're with Darius, it should be fine. Dorgaf is only three or four hours away by wagon, and the road isn't especially dangerous.”

“Then—”

“All right. You can go.”

“Yes!”

As Arvel celebrated, a disgruntled voice came from beside him.

“It's not fair that only you get to go!”

Milica puffed out her cheeks.

“I want to go too!”

“I'm only going along while Darius restocks his shop.”

“But you're going to Dorgaf!”

She clearly had no intention of accepting that explanation.

Sallis, who had been listening to their exchange, came over to Milica.

“Then, Milica, why don't you make some sweets with Mother today?”

“Sweets?”

The eyes that had been glaring at Arvel immediately turned toward her mother.

“Yes.”

“...Something sweet?”

“Of course.”

Milica thought for a while.

“...Then I don't mind.”

Sallis giggled at her answer.

Arvel returned to his room, put his spending money into a leather pouch, and headed back to Darius.

◇

“Arvel!”

A familiar voice stopped him along the road.

He knew who it was without even turning around.

“Tora.”

“Where are you going?”

Tora approached and looked curiously at Arvel, who was dressed to go out.

“I was thinking of asking you to go fishing.”

“I'm going to Dorgaf with Darius today.”

“Dorgaf!?”

As expected, Tora seized on the news.

“Why!?”

“I'm going to look for the second volume of The Tale of Hero Klaus. Darius is going there to restock, so he said he'd take me with him.”

“I'm going too!”

“What?”

“I'm going too!”

“Why did you say it twice...?”

When the two reached the general store, Darius was preparing to leave. He heard their story and laughed heartily.

“Sure, that's fine. Go get permission from the captain.”

“Got it!”

Tora immediately ran off.

“...He's as lively as ever.”

Darius chuckled as he watched Tora's back recede into the distance.

A short while later...

“He said I can go!”

A shout came from down the road, followed by Tora sprinting toward them at tremendous speed.

“That was fast...”

Arvel muttered without thinking.

◇

And so the wagon carrying the two boys left Finridge behind.

At first, everything Arvel could see from the wagon bed was familiar.

The forest he knew. The road he had walked many times.

But after they had traveled for an hour, familiar sights had gradually disappeared from the surrounding landscape.

Arvel leaned out from the wagon bed and watched the scenery flow past.

He had never seen any of it before.

They had not gone especially far from Finridge. Even so, simply traveling through an unfamiliar landscape made his heart race.

Beside him, Tora was restlessly looking all around.

“Hey, Darius.”

“What is it?”

“What kind of town is Dorgaf?”

When Arvel asked, Darius answered from the driver's seat.

“Let's see. There are far more people than in Finridge. Even so, it's a safe town.”

“Do you go there often?”

“I need to buy stock, after all. Besides, Dorgaf is my mother's hometown.”

“Céline's?”

“Yes.”

Arvel found that a little surprising.

He had never really thought about the fact that Céline had not been born in Finridge.

“What else is there?”

This time it was Tora who asked.

“What else? There are far more shops than in Finridge, and—oh, there's an Adventurers' Guild too.”

“An Adventurers' Guild!?”

Their voices overlapped.

Darius turned around in surprise.

“What's gotten into you all of a sudden?”

“There really is one!?”

“Of course there is.”

“Are there adventurers too?”

“Yes.”

“What about mages?”

“I imagine so.”

Darius gave a wry smile at the questions coming one after another.

“You two were staring at the scenery until a moment ago. Now you've suddenly gotten noisy.”

Even after he said that, their excitement did not easily subside.

To Arvel, adventurers were people who existed beyond the pages of books and the stories he heard from others.

And the town they were approaching had a place where those adventurers gathered.

Before long, Arvel had even forgotten to watch the passing scenery as he listened intently to Darius. Even Tora sat listening without interrupting, which was unusual for him.

Eventually, the wagon began to slow.

“Look. You can see it now.”

At Darius's words, both boys looked ahead.

Beyond the road stood a gate unlike anything they had ever seen in Finridge.

“Is that... Dorgaf?”

“Yes.”

As they approached the gate, more people and wagons appeared around them.

One of the guards stationed at the entrance recognized Darius and raised a hand.

“Darius! Restocking again today?”

“Yes. The usual.”

They were evidently acquainted. After a brief check, the guard let the wagon pass.

The wheels slowly rolled through the gate.

Arvel looked beyond it and caught his breath.

Dorgaf—the first town he had ever visited.

The “world outside,” which until now he had known only through books, was about to open before his eyes.
