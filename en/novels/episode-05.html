Episode Five: Awe

After their encounter with the owlbear, Arvel and the others confront the meaning of fear.

Episode Five: Awe

Story

After they had left the abandoned mine and walked through the forest for a while, Kieval spoke.

“What did you think?”

Tora let out a long breath.

“...There was no way we could handle that.”

At the uncharacteristic answer, Arvel gave a small nod as well.

“Me neither. Even from that far away, I couldn't move.”

That enormous body.

The overwhelming presence that seemed to shake him to his very core.

Merely remembering it made Arvel shudder all over again.

“That's good.”

Kieval spoke.

“That's what I wanted you to learn today.”

“To be afraid?”

Arvel asked.

“Yes. To know when you're facing something you can't do anything about.”

Kieval continued as they walked.

“Having a sword doesn't mean you can fight anything. If you meet an opponent you can't defeat, don't fight. If you can run, run. That's the best choice.”

Tora looked up at Kieval walking beside him.

“Weren't you scared, Kieval?”

“I'm always scared.”

At the unexpected answer, Arvel and Tora both turned to look at him.

Even before such a terrifying owlbear, Kieval had calmly watched and judged its behavior.

Hearing those words from his father came as a slight surprise to Arvel.

“Because I'm afraid, I watch my opponent. What it's trying to do, how close I can get before it becomes dangerous. What's frightening is when you stop thinking about those things.”

“...I see.”

Tora nodded with an expression that suggested he understood—or perhaps did not.

After they had walked a little farther, he spoke as though something had just occurred to him.

“Come to think of it, my dad told me once that owlbear cubs sell for a lot, so bandits and adventurers target them.”

“Yes.”

Kieval nodded.

“Then that one was protecting its cub too, right?”

“Most likely.”

Arvel remembered the small cub peering at them from behind the owlbear's body.

“Is that why it was so angry?”

“At the very least, it had no intention of chasing us.”

Kieval answered.

“When I backed away, it didn't leave the mine entrance either. It was probably trying to drive us off to protect its cub.”

“But why did it come all the way out here?”

Tora asked.

“I don't know.”

Kieval shook his head.

“Owlbears are intelligent monsters. They know humans target their young. Under normal circumstances, it's hard to imagine one coming this close to a settlement with a cub.”

“Maybe it was running from someone?”

“Perhaps. But for now, there's no way to say.”

At Kieval's answer, Tora did not press the matter any further.

The owlbear they had thought of as a terrifying monster had been protecting its child, just as they would.

Arvel could not get the image out of his mind: the owlbear standing at the mine entrance, concealing the cub behind it.

Strong and terrifying.

But that did not mean it attacked without reason.

Thinking about that, Arvel walked with Kieval and Tora along the road leading back to Finridge Village.

◇

By the time they returned to Finridge Village, the sun had already begun to sink.

Soft light from the west shone across the village roofs, and villagers could be seen in the fields finishing their work for the day.

The three passed through the village gate and headed straight for the village watch station.

When they stepped inside, Barks was waiting.

“You're back.”

“Yeah.”

“How did it go?”

“It was there. Just as we'd heard, it was an owlbear—and a higher-ranked specimen at that.”

Barks's expression tightened.

“Did it speak?”

“Yeah. It warned us not to come any closer.”

At the brief reply, Barks furrowed his brow.

“So, did you kill it?”

“No. But there's no need to worry.”

Kieval answered calmly.

“It had a cub. It was probably there to protect its young. As long as we don't provoke it, it won't attack us.”

“A cub...”

Barks folded his arms and considered that for a while.

“...Well, if that's your judgment, I'm sure it'll be all right.”

After saying that, he put a hand to his chin and thought again.

“The trail dead-ends beyond there. People only go as far as that area to gather wild plants, and almost no one ventures farther in.”

“Then are we going to leave it alone?”

Arvel asked.

“No. I'll tell Ruben, just in case. We should also warn the villagers to stay away from that area for a while.”

“What about subjugating it?”

This time, Tora asked.

“We won't.”

Barks answered without hesitation.

“If it won't attack unless we make the first move, there's no reason to provoke it. And if the owlbear settles there, that may not be entirely bad for us.”

“Why not?”

Tora tilted his head.

Arvel looked at Barks in the same way.

“They're omnivores. They eat nuts and plants, but they also prey on monsters like fluffers and wild boars.”

“They eat boars too?”

“Yes.”

Barks nodded.

“If the owlbear hunts the fluffers for us, I won't have to help Tora anymore.”

“H-hey... Arvel.”

Tora wore an expression that was difficult to describe.

“If it reduces the number of fluffers damaging the fields, that helps us too. As long as it doesn't harm the village, there's no reason to go out of our way to subjugate it.”

Just when it seemed the matter had been settled, Barks suddenly tilted his head.

“Still... why did an owlbear with a cub come all the way out here?”

“No idea. I don't know either.”

“I see... Well, I suppose we'll watch it for a while.”

Barks did not pursue the question further and let out a quiet breath, his arms still folded.

At that moment, a comically loud growl broke the silence.

When Arvel and Kieval turned, Tora was clutching his stomach.

“...I'm hungry.”

“You have no sense of tension at all.”

Barks said with an exasperated look.

“We've been moving around since morning. Of course I'm hungry.”

“I still have patrol duty. Tora, you go home ahead of me.”

“All right!”

At the cheerful reply, which made it hard to believe they had been discussing an owlbear moments ago, Kieval laughed.

“Arvel, shall we head home too?”

“Yeah.”

“See you, Arvel. Tomorrow!”

“See you tomorrow.”

Listening to Tora's voice, now back to its usual tone, Arvel left the watch station with Kieval.
