Episode One: A Small Achievement

Arvel and Tora discover an overturned carriage on the road outside Finridge Village.

Episode One: A Small Achievement

Story

“Hey, are you listening?”

Arvel did not answer Tora's call.

“Let's go tell our fathers. Come on, Arvel.”

“Shh.”

Arvel raised an index finger to his lips.

“...What is it?”

“I can hear something.”

As he spoke, he stared intently toward the carriage. Tora followed his lead and fell silent.

The sound of grass swaying in the wind. The rustle of leaves rubbing together in the trees. The two listened closely without speaking.

“...Ugh...”

“Huh?”

Tora furrowed his brow.

“That just now—”

“It's a person!”

They exchanged a glance, then raced toward the overturned carriage.

Wooden crates and burlap sacks lay scattered around the carriage that had left the road. As they drew closer, they saw people lying beside it.

“Hey! Are you all right?”

Tora shouted.

“...Ugh... nn...”

A faint voice answered. They were alive. Arvel looked around. This was not a situation the two of them could handle alone.

“Tora. Go get my father or the captain.”

“Right!”

Tora was about to run when Arvel immediately called him back.

“Wait!”

“What?”

“...Actually, I'll go.”

“Huh?”

Tora stared blankly for a moment, then quickly let out an “Oh.”

“I see. Then I'll stay here and keep watch.”

“Okay. I'll be right back!”

Arvel dashed out onto the road. He gathered wind around his feet. The instant he kicked off the ground, he released that wind behind him. His body surged forward. One step. Then another. Each time the wind pushed at his back, his running speed increased. He tore through the forest and raced down the road toward the village.

◇

“Captain!”

The moment he burst into the watch station, Arvel shouted. Alerted by his voice, Barks Harland, captain of the village watch, turned around.

“What happened, Arvel? Why are you in such a hurry?”

“A carriage overturned! On the road outside the village! There are people down too, and one of them is still conscious!”

Barks's expression changed.

“Where?”

“On the western road! A little way past the forest!”

“Where's Tora?”

“He stayed at the scene!”

“Understood.”

Barks immediately called out to a nearby watchman.

“Gather several people! We're heading to the western road. Bring a stretcher and anything we can use for first aid!”

“Yes, sir!”

The watchman ran off.

“Arvel.”

“Yeah!”

“Go to Celine. We'll be bringing injured people in. Have her prepare the clinic to receive them.”

“Got it!”

“We'll go on ahead!”

Arvel nodded and dashed out of the station again.

◇

“Celine!”

Arvel opened the clinic door.

“Oh? Arvel, what is it?”

“There are injured people!”

Celine’s expression changed.

“Stay calm. What happened?”

“A carriage had overturned on the road, and there were people lying there too. I think there were three. One of them spoke, but the other two... I don’t know.”

“Three, then.”

Celine immediately sprang into action.

“Did you tell Barks?”

“Yeah. He’s already heading there with some of the watchmen.”

“Understood. I’ll get ready.”

As she spoke, Celine began taking the necessary supplies from the shelves.

“Then I’m going back to Tora!”

“All right. Be careful.”

Arvel dashed out of the clinic.

When Arvel returned to the accident site, several members of the village watch had already gathered there.

Beside the overturned carriage, Barks and his men were tending to the injured.

“Arvel!”

It was Tora who called out.

“Tora! What about the other two?”

“They’re all right.”

Barks was the one who answered.

“They’re only unconscious. None of the three is in any danger of dying.”

“Thank goodness...”

Arvel let out a breath of relief.

“What about Celine?”

“I told her. She’s preparing the clinic.”

At that, Barks looked at Arvel.

“...You’re back already?”

“Yeah.”

For a moment, Barks gave Arvel a puzzled look. Then he promptly turned his attention back to the injured.

“Arvel!”

Arvel turned at the familiar voice.

“Dad!”

Kieval came running down the road. “Are you hurt?”

“No. Tora and I are both fine.”

“I see.”

Once he had confirmed that much, Kieval turned toward the overturned carriage.

“Barks, what’s the situation?”

“Three people. They’re injured, but none of them is in danger of dying.”

“Understood. What should I do?”

“Help us here until the stretchers arrive.”

Before long, watchmen carrying stretchers came running from farther down the road.

“Captain! We brought them!”

“Good. Over here.”

At Barks’s direction, the watchmen placed the injured on the stretchers one at a time. The watchmen carried the three stretchers toward the village. Arvel and Tora watched until they receded into the distance down the road.

“...I’m glad.”

“Yeah.”

As though the tension had finally left him, Tora exhaled deeply.

After watching the three injured people being carried to the clinic, Kieval turned his attention to the cargo scattered across the road.

We'll need a cart if we're going to carry all of this.

Barks nodded as well.

Yeah, you're right. Let's head back to the village for now.

The four gathered the scattered cargo beside the road, then began walking toward the village.

After they had walked for a while, Barks suddenly glanced at Tora.

...Come to think of it, Tora.

Hm?

I thought I asked you to hunt Fluffers?

........

Tora's steps slowed slightly.

I-I did hunt the Fluffers properly.

Really? You hunted creatures that fast on their feet in such a short time?

I-I'm telling the truth! We really did hunt them! ...Only two, though.

Two!? You were slacking off again!

Barks raised his fist.

W-wait, wait! We really did hunt them! Right, Arvel?

Suddenly drawn into the conversation, Arvel blinked.

Yeah. We found that carriage just after we took down two of them.

See! I told you!

Kieval, who had been watching the exchange, cut in with a laugh.

Hey, Barks. Whatever else happened... these two deserve the credit today.

...Well, that's true enough.

Barks lowered his raised fist and questioned him no further. The four resumed their walk. But after a while, Barks suddenly tilted his head.

...That reminds me, Arvel.

What?

You came back awfully quickly after going to tell Celine, didn't you?

Oh, that?

You went to the clinic first, right? Yet you got back before the men who went to fetch the stretcher.

Walking beside them, Tora gave a sly grin. Seeing his expression, Kieval shifted his gaze to Arvel.

...Did you do something?

Yeah. I used magic.

Kieval's eyes widened in surprise.

Magic? Did your mother teach you?

No. It was everyday magic.

Everyday magic?

Yeah. Tora is always asking me to help him hunt Fluffers. They're fast runners, so I adapted everyday magic.

The smile vanished smoothly from Tora's face as he walked beside him.

...Always?

Tora's shoulders jerked at Barks's low voice. Arvel, however, continued without appearing to notice.

So I thought I might be able to run faster if I used wind to push my body from behind.

W-wait a second, Arvel!

Huh? ...Oh.

Arvel finally realized what he had said and looked awkwardly at Tora.

...Tora.

Barks's low voice echoed. Tora's shoulders jerked.

You little...!

I-it's not what you think, Dad! There's a reason for this—!

Before he could finish, Tora took off running toward the village.

Wait, Tora!

Watching his back rapidly recede into the distance, Barks let out a deep sigh.

Honestly, that little troublemaker...

Even as he said it, Barks wore a somewhat exasperated smile. Kieval burst out laughing, and Arvel could not help laughing with him.
