Episode Seven: Town Life

On their first visit to Dorgaf, Arvel and his companions experience the bustle of town and discover new uses for goods from Finridge.

Episode Seven: Town Life

Story

The moment they passed through the gate, a scene utterly unlike anything before spread out before Arvel's eyes.

Buildings lined both sides of the road, and crowds of people moved between them.

Some hurried along carrying packages. Merchants called to customers from their storefronts. Others pulled carts through the streets.

Voices and laughter rose from every direction, joined now and then by a shopkeeper's vigorous cry.

There were more people than he had ever seen in Finridge.

“Whoa...!”

Tora leaned out from the wagon bed.

Beside him, Arvel kept glancing eagerly from one side to the other.

Even the road beneath them was different.

In the village, the roads were bare earth. Here, flat stones had been laid together to form a paved street.

“Arvel, look over there!”

“Yeah. But there's something over here too.”

There was so much to see that neither of them knew where to look.

With the two boys still aboard, the wagon slowly made its way through town, rattling over the stone pavement.

After traveling along the main street for a while, Darius finally pulled on the reins.

The wagon slowed and stopped in front of a building.

Arvel looked up at it from the wagon bed.

Above the entrance hung a sign depicting what appeared to be medicinal herbs.

“An alchemy workshop...”

Arvel murmured softly.

“This is our first stop. Sorry, boys, but could you help me unload?”

“Sure!”

“Leave it to me!”

As the two climbed down from the wagon, the workshop door opened.

The man who emerged saw Darius and immediately looked past him at Arvel and Tora.

“Hey, Darius. Brought two underlings with you today?”

“Ha! No, no. I'm just helping these two with some shopping today.”

“Oh, is that so?”

The man laughed in amusement.

Several wooden crates brought from Finridge were stacked in the wagon bed.

The scent of dried herbs drifted through the gaps in their lids, while other crates held neatly arranged bottles of potions and medicines.

“These go over here. Be careful with the crates containing bottles.”

At Darius's instruction, Arvel and Tora each lifted a crate.

When they carried the crates inside, they found shelves lined with bottles of every size, while dried plants and unfamiliar materials filled every available space.

Farther inside stood an assortment of tools that seemed to be used for compounding mixtures.

Arvel nearly stopped in his tracks, but—

“Arvel, next one!”

Tora's voice from outside brought him back to himself.

“Coming!”

The three made several trips, and before long they had carried in everything intended for the alchemy workshop.

Meanwhile, Darius and the proprietor inspected the goods and discussed payment and the next delivery.

“All right. That's everything here.”

With the transaction complete, Darius returned to the wagon.

“Is there more?”

“Yes. We're making several stops today.”

They climbed back aboard and continued along the paved street.

The next place they visited was an inn.

What they unloaded there was neither herbs nor potions.

“Is this fluffer?”

Arvel asked as he looked at a wrapped bundle in the wagon bed.

“Yes. The meat.”

“They use it at the inn?”

Tora asked.

“They cook with it. It has a mild flavor, so apparently it's quite versatile.”

The three carried the wrapped meat through the inn's back entrance.

Once they had finished, they headed to a tailor's shop. There, they unloaded beautifully prepared pelts of pure white fur.

“Oh, this is fluffer too.”

Arvel recognized it at once.

“These are the pelts.”

Darius held one up.

“Fluffer fur is warm. It's well suited to winter clothing, so it sells especially well when the weather starts turning cold.”

“Huh...”

Tora touched the fur.

“They're a nuisance in the fields, but they're actually pretty useful.”

“You can eat the meat and use the fur. Even their magic stones can be sold.”

As he spoke, Darius shifted the bundle of pelts in his arms.

“Besides, there are more fluffers around Finridge than in most other places.”

“Really?”

“They like nutritious medicinal herbs. Finridge has plenty of those, doesn't it?”

Hearing that, Arvel and Tora looked at each other.

“Oh...”

“So that's why!”

Tora cried out.

“That's why we have to drive fluffers out of the fields so often!”

“Because there are so many medicinal herbs...”

“Exactly.”

Darius laughed.

Arvel pictured the white monsters he had chased away so many times before.

He had always thought of them as nothing more than pests that ruined the fields. Yet here in town, both their meat and fur were sold as useful goods.

It seemed that things taken for granted in Finridge could serve different purposes beyond the village.

◇

By the time they had finished all their deliveries, it was already a little past noon.

After unloading the last of the goods, Darius checked the now-empty wagon bed and looked at the boys.

“Well then. Shall we get something to eat?”

“Yes!”

Tora answered at once.

Arvel nodded enthusiastically too.

“I'm hungry too.”

“You've been helping me since this morning. You can have anything you like.”

“Really!?”

“Yes.”

The three tied the wagon at a nearby hitching post and entered a restaurant facing the main street.

The moment they opened the door, a lively roar of conversation rushed to meet them.

The dining room was crowded with people having lunch.

There were craftsmen who looked to be taking a break from work, men dressed for travel, and even families.

When the three found an open table and sat down, Arvel and Tora immediately began looking at the tables around them.

“I wonder what they have.”

“What kind of food do they eat in Dorgaf?”

It was their first meal in a town they had never visited before.

Both boys expected to see dishes unlike anything they had ever encountered in Finridge.

But as they watched plates being carried past, their expressions gradually began to change.

“...Huh?”

Tora tilted his head.

“It all looks pretty ordinary.”

“Yeah...”

Meat and vegetables cooked in a stew.

Roasted meat.

Bread.

Soup.

The ingredients and seasonings might have differed slightly, but none of it was much different from the food they knew in Finridge.

Darius burst out laughing when he saw their faces.

“What? Did you think you'd get to eat something more unusual?”

“But it's a town!”

Tora protested.

“And it's way bigger than Finridge.”

“It is bigger, but Finridge is only about three hours away by wagon. They're neighbors in the same region of Bermouthia. The food isn't going to change completely all of a sudden.”

“I guess not...”

Arvel seemed oddly satisfied by the explanation, while Tora beside him still looked a little disappointed.

“Just try it. Even the same dish tastes different from one restaurant to another.”

Before long, plates of meat and root vegetables slowly simmered together arrived before the three, accompanied by freshly baked bread.

An appetizing aroma rose with the steam.

“Let's eat!”

Tora, who had looked disappointed only moments before, was the first to pick up his spoon.

His expression changed the instant he took a bite.

“This is great!”

“In the end, you'd be happy with anything, wouldn't you?”

Darius laughed.

Arvel tried the stew as well.

It was certainly a familiar kind of dish.

Yet perhaps because the spices were used differently, or because of the way it had been simmered, it tasted unlike the meals he ate at home or at Hearthroot.

“You're right. It's delicious.”

“See?”

Their first lunch in Dorgaf was not as unusual as they had imagined.

Even so, the two boys filled their empty stomachs while excitedly talking about everything they had seen and heard in town since morning.
