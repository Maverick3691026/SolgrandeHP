Lake Negreia

Lake Negreia

A sacred alpine lake cradled by mountains, where the blessing of the creator goddess Liviana remains strong even today.

Basic Information

Name
Lake Negreia
English Name
Lake Negreia
Classification
Lake and Natural Landscape
Location
An alpine lake in the mountains south of Laudan, southeastern Principality of Bermouthia

Overview

An alpine lake cradled by mountains, known as a sacred lake where the blessing of the creator goddess Liviana remains strong.

The blessing prevents monsters from entering the surrounding area, making the lakeshore a paradise inhabited by small animals and a great variety of plants.

Laudan Village stands by the lake, where an altar and stone statue dedicated to Liviana rest quietly.

History and Origin

Lake Negreia is said to be the land where Liviana settled the first humans, whom she created in her own likeness.

This tradition remains deeply preserved in Laudan Village, where faith in Liviana is rooted in everyday life.

The people now living in Laudan are said to be descendants of those first humans.

Ancient writings believed to record the age of creation have also been passed down through generations and are kept under strict protection by successive village chiefs.

Highlights

The mystical landscape of clear water surrounded by mountains is one of Heiramnova's foremost scenic places and attracts many pilgrims and travelers.

The stone statue of Liviana standing quietly by the lake is another symbol of the area.

Culture and Customs

Faith in Liviana is deeply rooted in life at Laudan, and villagers customarily pray at the church morning and evening.

The creation traditions and ancient writings are treasured by the village, and learning the creation myth from childhood forms part of local education.

Once each year, the village holds a Creation Festival before the statue of Liviana overlooking the lake.

Liviana's followers and historians from many regions visit, joining villagers in bringing alcohol and food as offerings and giving thanks for the year's harvest and peace.

A feast follows, and participants spend the night singing and talking as they offer gratitude to the creator goddess.

Dangers

Liviana's blessing keeps monsters from entering, and the area is known as one of the safest in Heiramnova.

Some parts of the lakeshore and nearby mountain paths have poor footing, so visitors must still take care.

Access

The usual route to Lake Negreia passes through Laudan Village.

A maintained path continues from the village to the lake, which can be reached easily on foot.

Travelers, historians, and followers of Liviana use Laudan as their base when visiting.

A mountain road also links the area with Chipton in the Fortress Principality of Tarrenfall, but confirmed monster activity makes that route dangerous; ordinary travelers are advised to use the road through Laudan.
