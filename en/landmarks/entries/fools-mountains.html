Fools' Mountains

Fools' Mountains

Part of an immense mountain range crossing the continent of Brenoria.

Basic Information

Name
Fools' Mountains
English Name
Fools' Mountains
Classification
Natural Landscape

Overview

The name used for the mountain range around the Fortress Principality of Tarrenfall, the Principality of Bermouthia, and the mining city of Kazandor.

It refers to part of the immense range crossing the continent of Brenoria, which bears different names in different regions.

History and Origin

Not yet established.

Highlights

The mountains are extremely high, and their upper slopes and summits remain covered in snow and ice throughout the year.

The magnificent scenery formed by sheer rocky peaks and deep valleys attracts many travelers.

Dangers

Alongside the harsh terrain, sudden weather changes and avalanches can occur at high elevations.

Monsters inhabit the range, making a crossing difficult without adequate equipment and experience.

Access

Mountain paths extend from regional roads, but each ends within the range; no route crosses it.

Powerful monsters such as wyverns and dragons nest in some areas, which should not be entered without adequate equipment and ability.

Notes

Even the same range has different names by region.

In the Kingdom of Svarn it is called the Silver-Ridge Mountains, after the year-round snow on its peaks that gleams silver in sunlight.

In the kingdoms of Sinestadia and Garmentia it is called the Lovers' Mountains, because the range's two highest peaks resemble lovers nestled together.
