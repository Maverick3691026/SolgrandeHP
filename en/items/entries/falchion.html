Overview

A cutting-focused one-handed sword with a broad single-edged blade. It combines an axe-like powerful blow with a sword’s mobility. Its forward center of gravity gives descending cuts great force against lightly armored enemies and monsters. Its practicality has made it common among soldiers, mercenaries, and adventurers, with many regional forms.

Appearance and Structure

Its overall length is about 2 lieves 8 til to 3 lieves 2 til. The blade broadens toward the point, placing its balance forward for strong cuts. Most are single-edged and transfer their weight efficiently into each blow.

Functions and Effects

It delivers powerful cuts, strikes armor gaps and monster hides with heavy impact, and is comparatively easy for beginners to learn.

History

The falchion began as a machete used by settlers clearing forests. Repeated monster attacks drove its refinement from work tool to defensive weapon, ultimately joining a sword’s agility with an axe’s destructive power. Nations recognized its practicality and adopted it for soldiers and mercenaries. Advances in smithing refined its blade and balance, making it a representative cutting sword now made throughout Elamnova.

Current Availability

Throughout Elamnova.

Related People

Notes

It suits cutting-centered combat and its forward balance strengthens each blow. Some weapons of this kind are said to descend from agricultural or working tools.
