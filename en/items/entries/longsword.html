The most widespread one-handed sword across Elamnova.

Overview

The most widespread one-handed sword across Elamnova. Its excellent balance of attack, defense, and handling makes it popular with adventurers, knights, and soldiers. It is accessible to beginners while still answering the skill of an expert.

Appearance and Structure

Its overall length is about 3 lieves to 3 lieves 4 til. The straight, slender double-edged blade supports both cuts and thrusts. A central ridge strengthens it, and artisans often add decorative engraving near the center. The grip is carefully wrapped in leather, the horizontal crossguard catches enemy blades, and the leather-covered sheath has reinforcing fittings and a leather fastener for the belt.

Functions and Effects

It balances offense and defense, suits cutting and thrusting, and can be used by everyone from beginners to masters. It also accepts magical and elemental enchantments well and serves as the basis of many magic swords.

History

Longswords developed long ago as the main arms of knights and soldiers. Smiths throughout the world now produce them, making them one of the most common swords in weapon shops. Their refined design underlies many famous and magical blades.

Current Availability

Throughout Elamnova.

Related People

Notes

They are standard equipment for adventurers, knights, and soldiers and are frequently used for training at knight schools. Quality varies greatly with materials and smithing skill. More derivative and magical weapons are based on this sword type than any other.
