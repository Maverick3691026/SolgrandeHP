A short blade wielded in one hand.

Overview

A short blade wielded in one hand. Easy to carry, it serves for self-defense, as a secondary weapon, and for dismantling harvested creatures. It is easy for beginners and veterans alike to use and is one of the most widespread daggers in Elamnova.

Appearance and Structure

Its overall length is about 1 lieve 5 til. It has a slender double-edged blade and a sharp point suited to thrusting. A central ridge strengthens the blade, while the guard curves gently outward to protect the hand. The leather-wrapped grip provides a secure hold. Its matching leather sheath is fastened firmly at the waist with metal fittings and leather straps.

Functions and Effects

It can be drawn quickly with one hand, supports both cuts and thrusts, is convenient for everyday carry, and works well as a medium for magical or elemental enchantment.

History

Daggers have existed since ancient times and continue to be made by many smiths. They are now sold widely in weapon shops throughout the regions and carried as secondary weapons by adventurers, soldiers, hunters, travelers, and many others.

Current Availability

Throughout Elamnova.

Related People

Notes

They are often recommended as beginner equipment, are useful for dismantling small monsters and gathering materials, and are commonly carried as everyday tools as well as weapons.
