A one-handed saber with a deeply curved blade.

Overview

A one-handed saber with a deeply curved blade. Its design specializes in cutting and excels at quick, fluid combinations. It suits mounted combat and mobile swordsmen and is known as a weapon developed across broad plains and deserts.

Appearance and Structure

Its overall length is about 2 lieves 10 til to 3 lieves 4 til. The blade curves strongly toward the point so its edge naturally bites into the target during a cut. One-handed balance enables light swings and successive attacks. Models range from practical arms to lavishly decorated pieces.

Functions and Effects

It delivers fast, smooth cuts, suits mounted and high-speed combat, and excels at combinations and sweeping attacks.

History

The shamshir is a one-handed curved sword originating in the Sand Kingdom of Sandrea. It developed among mounted peoples crossing the vast desert, and its blade gradually became more curved to maximize downward cuts from horseback. Other nations praised its cutting ability and mobility, and knights, mercenaries, adventurers, and many other warriors now use it.

Current Availability

Throughout Elamnova.

Related People

Notes

It is favored by mounted swordsmen, better suited to cutting than thrusting, and requires its own distinctive swordsmanship.
