Gladius

A highly maneuverable short sword.

Overview

A highly maneuverable short sword, well suited to confined spaces and formation fighting. Its quick one-handed handling makes it common among soldiers and adventurers. It combines strong thrusting ability with sufficient cutting power.

Appearance and Structure

Its overall length is about 1 lieve 11 til to 2 lieves 4 til. The broad, straight double-edged blade ends in a point shaped for thrusting. A ridge strengthens it, and its balance is adjusted for one-handed use.

Functions and Effects

It handles well in narrow spaces, supports both thrusts and cuts, and can be drawn quickly in sudden combat.

History

Martello, regarded as the first Dwarf King of Kazandor, established a technique for forge-welding high-carbon steel refined from pig iron with tough soft iron. The resulting sword combined a keen edge with high durability. Named for its birthplace, the “Gladius Kazandorensis” spread to other nations and became the model for the modern gladius.

Current Availability

Throughout Elamnova.

Related People

Notes

Known for being manageable by beginners and experts alike, it also suits combat styles built around a shield and is widely issued to light infantry and village watches.
