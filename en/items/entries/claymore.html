Overview

A two-handed sword combining tremendous power with long reach. It demands great strength and sound technique, but its blows exert exceptional force and control groups of enemies on the battlefield. It symbolizes seasoned warriors and heavily armored knights.

Appearance and Structure

Its overall length is about 4 lieves 5 til to 5 lieves 4 til. The long double-edged blade has powerful cutting and adequate thrusting ability, with weight and balance designed for two hands. Its extended grip provides secure, stable control even in prolonged battle. Models range from austere battlefield weapons to richly decorated pieces.

Functions and Effects

It delivers powerful cuts, exploits long reach, and remains formidable against heavy armor and large monsters. It also accepts magical and elemental enchantments well and forms the basis of many magical greatswords.

History

Large two-handed swords have served on battlefields since ancient times and were prized by many nations for overwhelming attack power. Smiths still make them across the world for capable knights, mercenaries, and adventurers.

Current Availability

Throughout Elamnova.

Related People

Notes

It requires great strength and training and assumes two-handed use without a shield. It controls heavily armored foes and large monsters effectively. Workshops vary in design and decoration, producing specifications for many fighting styles.
