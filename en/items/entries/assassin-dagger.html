Assassin Dagger

A specialized dagger designed for covert operations and special missions.

Overview

A specialized dagger designed for covert operations and special missions. Lighter and more mobile than an ordinary dagger, it favors fast draws, combinations, and precise thrusts to vital points. Its unusual blade and balance demand training and experience. Advanced smithing limits supply, so ordinary shops rarely stock it. Specialist workshops, black markets, and select arms dealers supply scouts, spies, assassins, and adventurers on special assignments.

Appearance and Structure

Its overall length is about 11 til to 1 lieve 3 til. The slender, sharp blade emphasizes thrusting while still supporting cuts. Balance near the grip aids rapid close-range combinations and reverse-grip use. A practical finish suppresses reflected light.

Functions and Effects

It excels at quick draws and successive attacks, supports thrusts and cuts, and suits stealth and close combat. Some models are designed for throwing.

History

The assassin dagger arose from requests by people engaged in infiltration, reconnaissance, and other special missions. Seeking silence and mobility beyond an ordinary dagger, smiths repeatedly refined its blade and balance into the present distinctive form. Its performance won high regard among a limited circle, but the skill required prevented broad adoption. Precise work by master smiths keeps production low, so it circulates mostly through specialist workshops and black markets. It is now valued both by covert professionals and by some adventurers with advanced close-combat skill.

Current Availability

Throughout Elamnova.

Related People

Notes

It is more often carried as a secondary weapon than a primary one. Schools use varied forward- and reverse-grip methods, and special fittings exist for concealed sheaths or mounting on the arms and legs.
