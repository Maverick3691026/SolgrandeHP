Overview

A longbow widely used throughout Elamnova. It excels at precise long-range shooting and serves in hunting and warfare. It requires strength and technique, but a skilled archer achieves excellent accuracy and penetration.

Appearance and Structure

Its overall length is about 5 lieves 4 til to 6 lieves. A flexible stave and durable string generate high tension. The stave uses wood combining durability and flexibility for long service. Grips, ornament, and styling vary greatly by maker and workshop, from purely practical bows to artistic pieces. Quivers also differ in shape and capacity and are usually carried at the waist or back.

Functions and Effects

It enables precise fire at long range, adapts to many situations through different arrows, and gains accuracy and effective range with mastery. It also takes magical and elemental enchantments well and forms the basis of many magic bows.

History

Bows developed as hunting tools in ancient times and were later adopted widely for war. Bowyers and woodworkers now make them throughout the world for hunters, adventurers, and soldiers. Their mature structure is the model for many celebrated and magical bows.

Current Availability

Throughout Elamnova.

Related People

Notes

Models exist for hunting, warfare, and competition. Arrows are consumable, so users normally carry spares. Performance depends on the combination of stave, string, and arrows. Workshops differ in design philosophy and ornament, dividing the preferences of experienced archers.
