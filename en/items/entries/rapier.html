Rapier

A slender one-handed sword designed mainly for thrusting.

Overview

A slender one-handed sword designed mainly for thrusting. Its mobility and precision make it popular among knights, nobles, and skilled swordsmen. Though demanding to master, an accurate strike has considerable stopping power.

Appearance and Structure

Its overall length is about 3 lieves 2 til to 3 lieves 9 til. The light, slender blade tracks straight and has a sharp point ideal for thrusts. A complex guard protects the wielder’s hand from enemy blades.

Functions and Effects

It excels at accurate thrusts, supports rapid movement and combinations, and favors evasion and parrying over direct blocking.

History

The rapier originated from the estoc, a two-handed thrusting sword once used on battlefields. Repeated refinement for peacetime self-defense and dueling produced today’s light, slender thrusting sword. Its structure, balance, and techniques differ greatly from the estoc, requiring dedicated training.

Current Availability

Throughout Elamnova.

Related People

Notes

Its effectiveness depends heavily on swordsmanship. It is favored by users who value exact distance and technique over brute force, and is often carried for dueling or self-defense. Many variants reflect the wielder’s school and preferences.
