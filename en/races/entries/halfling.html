Halfling

A small, dexterous, and patient people who cherish peaceful lives, festivals, and feasts.

Overview

Halflings are smaller than humans, standing around 120 centimeters tall, but excel in dexterity and patience and possess great skill in fields that support daily life, including farming, animal husbandry, and cooking.

Many are cheerful and sociable and love festivals and feasts above all else. Though peaceful and averse to conflict, they summon the courage to stand against danger when protecting beloved family and friends.

Physical Traits

Halflings stand around 120 centimeters tall. They are smaller than humans and excel in dexterity and patience.

Culture and Daily Life

Halflings build individual homes in nature-rich places such as hills and forests and lead peaceful lives. They excel at farming, animal husbandry, and cooking, and can be seen throughout the world teaching these skills to other races through their extensive knowledge and experience.

They also love festivals and feasts above all else and are known for enlivening celebrations with song and dance across racial boundaries.

Social Systems

Halflings have no social system unique to their race and live according to the laws and institutions of the nation or region in which they reside.

Abilities and Aptitudes

Halflings excel at farming, animal husbandry, and cooking. They are skilled at work that benefits from their dexterity and patience.

History

An old tradition holds that halflings are fairies who diminished over the course of many ages. No records or artifacts supporting this theory have been discovered, and its truth remains unknown.

Relations with Other Races

Halflings are generally friendly with other races, and their sincere and warm nature earns them affection among many peoples. They are also trusted for spreading agricultural and pastoral knowledge throughout the world.

Notes

Halflings possess no fixed villages or nation and live individually in nature-rich lands. They love festivals and feasts above all else. Many are pacifists, and their culture places great importance on family and companions.
