Race Detail

Elf

A long-lived race with exceptional magical aptitude, skilled in magic and archery.

None

Overview

Elves are a long-lived race with exceptional magical aptitude. They excel at magic, and many are also accomplished archers.

Both men and women are tall and refined in appearance, and their beauty is widely known among other races.

Because they have difficulty conceiving, their birth rate is low and their population is small. They place great importance on bonds with their kin and settlements.

They keep contact with the outside world to a minimum. Many elves build settlements deep within the Granzahl Forest Region and live secluded from the outside world behind magical barriers.

Physical Traits

Both men and women are tall and well proportioned. Many possess refined features. They have exceptional magical aptitude. They are long-lived but have a low birth rate.

Culture and Daily Life

Elves value harmony with nature above all else and build their settlements deep within the Granzahl Forest Region. They cherish bonds with blood relatives and members of their settlement and approach contact with the outside world cautiously. Those who enter the forest without permission almost never reach an elven settlement.

Social Systems

Each settlement is governed as a community centered on its elder. Important decisions are not made by the elder alone; the views of influential members are also respected, creating a society that values both tradition and consensus.

Abilities and Aptitudes

Elves possess exceptionally high magical aptitude and excel at archery. Their long lives allow knowledge and techniques to be passed down through generations.

History

Many theories exist regarding the origins of elves, but no reliable historical records have yet been discovered, and the details remain unknown.

Relations with Other Races

Elves generally have little contact with other races and maintain a certain distance from them.

At the same time, their beauty and rarity often make them targets for kidnappers and black-market traders, further strengthening their wariness of the outside world.

Differences in values sometimes bring them into conflict with dwarves, but not all are hostile, and some acknowledge and respect one another's knowledge and skill.

Notes

Half-elves exist. Dark elves whose skin has turned gray under the influence of dark magic or necromancy also exist. Their rarity can command high prices on the black market, making them targets for kidnappers. Elven is a distinct language with consonant mutations and a sound reminiscent of Welsh.
