Beastfolk

A collective name for many peoples who inherit the traits of animals.

Overview

Beastfolk is a collective name for many peoples who inherit animal traits, including catfolk, wolffolk, and tigerfolk. Physical abilities and areas of expertise differ greatly between them. Catfolk excel at agility and stealth, while wolffolk are skilled at coordination within a pack and tracking; each people has its own distinctive traits.

Many are friendly and loyal, and beastfolk adventurers, mercenaries, and merchants are a common sight throughout the world.

Physical Traits

Each people has different physical traits, such as animal ears or tails. Physical ability and the five senses vary greatly, as does lifespan, though many live about as long as humans or somewhat less.

Culture and Daily Life

Culture and lifestyle differ greatly between individual peoples. Some have ancestral settlements, while many live within the societies of humans and other races and work throughout the world as adventurers, mercenaries, or merchants.

Social Systems

The ancestral homeland of each people has its own social systems and customs. These systems generally do not apply to beastfolk who live in the towns and villages of humans or other races.

Abilities and Aptitudes

Each beastfolk people possesses its own exceptional physical abilities. Catfolk excel at agility and stealth. Wolffolk excel at pack coordination and tracking. Tigerfolk and several other peoples possess exceptional strength and combat ability.

History

Beastfolk are not a single race but a collective of many peoples. Their origins and traditions therefore differ, and they share no single common history.

Relations with Other Races

Beastfolk are generally friendly with other races and build relationships throughout the world. Some, however, misuse their physical gifts as thieves or criminals, and this can make them objects of suspicion in certain regions.

Notes

Beastfolk is a collective term encompassing many peoples rather than a single race. Catfolk, wolffolk, tigerfolk, and others each possess different physical abilities and cultures. Their homelands, lifestyles, and social systems vary greatly.
