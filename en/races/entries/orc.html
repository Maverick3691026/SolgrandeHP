Orc

A powerfully built race known for woodworking and construction.

Overview

Orcs are a muscular, powerfully built race with large tusks rising from the lower jaw. Their appearance often inspires fear, but many are in fact gentle and loyal and form friendly relationships with other races.

Many work as lumberjacks or woodworkers and possess excellent construction skills. Their sturdy wooden buildings and bridges are highly valued by nations throughout the world.

Physical Traits

Orcs have muscular, powerful builds and large tusks that grow from the lower jaw. They possess exceptional strength and endurance.

Culture and Daily Life

Orcs establish settlements throughout Solgrande and live by making use of forest resources. Many work as lumberjacks or woodworkers and excel at construction, particularly sturdy wooden buildings and bridges. Their skill is highly regarded by nations throughout the world.

Social Systems

Scattered settlements form tribal communities led by chieftains and guided by tradition and custom. This system does not apply to orcs who live in the towns and villages of humans or other races.

Abilities and Aptitudes

Orcs possess exceptional strength and endurance and excel at woodworking and construction. Many serve on the front line in battle. When protecting family or companions, they bring their powerful bodies and tremendous strength fully to bear.

History

It is said that orcs were once feared for their intimidating appearance and persecuted in many regions.

A tradition still tells of a human warrior who protected them during that age and showed them a path toward living together, beginning their friendly relationship with humanity. No surviving records confirm the story, however, and whether it is historical fact remains uncertain.

Relations with Other Races

Orcs are generally friendly with other races and interact with many nations through their construction and woodworking skills. Although their appearance can lead to misunderstanding and prejudice, many are gentle and loyal, and their relationship with humans is especially strong.

Notes

Many orcs are gentle despite their appearance. Only a small number become adventurers. Their construction and woodworking techniques rank among the finest in Solgrande.
