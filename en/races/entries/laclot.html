Laclot

A humanoid people of great stature and profound intellect, devoted to the pursuit and preservation of knowledge.

Overview

The Laclot are a humanoid people who combine great stature with profound intellect.

They founded Rezul, the world's foremost city of scholarship, and regard the protection of knowledge and its transmission to the future as their people's mission.

They are well versed in history and magical studies and continue their research alongside scholars who visit from nations across the world.

Their wisdom is broadly open to the world, while certain books remain under strict control, quietly helping to preserve the world's balance.

Physical Traits

The Laclot are humanoids whose physiques are more than a size larger than those of humans.

Both men and women possess muscular, sturdy bodies and are distinguished by brown skin and broad, straight noses.

Their ears are slightly long and pointed, while their composed features convey deep intelligence and dignity.

Their hands, feet, and fingers are large, granting them great physical strength and a powerful grip.

At the same time, their movements are surprisingly delicate, allowing them to perform fine work such as copying manuscripts and conducting magical research with ease.

Culture and Daily Life

Laclot culture values the protection of knowledge and its transmission to the future above all else.

From childhood they study a broad range of subjects, including history, magical studies, astronomy, and botany, and it is considered virtuous to deepen their knowledge throughout life.

They welcome scholars and researchers from around the world and value advancing research through the mutual exchange of knowledge.

Nations often ask them to copy and restore old documents, authenticate historical materials, and investigate ancient ruins, and their exceptional learning is trusted throughout the world.

When greeting someone for the first time, a Laclot introduces not only their own name but also those of their father and grandfather, saying, for example, “It is a pleasure to meet you. I am [name], son of [name], who was in turn the son of [name].”

This custom expresses respect not for bloodline itself but for the parents and ancestors through whom knowledge has been inherited. It endures as a distinctive Laclot tradition not found in other nations.

Social Systems

The Independent City of Rezul, home of the Laclot, is a self-governing city that values knowledge and neutrality.

Political and other important matters are deliberated and decided by a council of elders and scholars representing fields such as history, magical studies, law, and medicine.

The city maintains friendly relations with nations throughout the world and preserves its independence as a neutral city belonging to none of them.

Because of its scholarly value, Rezul has concluded nonaggression treaties with other nations, creating an environment that scholars and researchers from around the world can visit in safety.

Although knowledge is understood as something that should be shared broadly with the world, books whose circulation is restricted are kept under a rigorous system of control and entrusted only to a limited number of custodians.

Abilities and Aptitudes

The Laclot are born with exceptional intelligence and strong memories and possess great aptitude for scholarship in general, particularly history and magical studies. Their long lives also make it common for an individual to pursue a single field for more than a century.

Their powerful bodies and exceptional strength also suit them to transporting heavy books and stone monuments and to investigating ruins.

Their large hands might appear poorly suited to precision work, but their concentration and dexterity allow them to copy manuscripts and restore documents to an exceptionally high standard.

They also possess strong magical aptitude. Many specialize in support magic that applies knowledge through analysis, enhancement, sealing, and similar disciplines.

History

An ancient tradition holds that the god Emtis created the Laclot to bestow knowledge upon humankind. No reliable records proving this account survive, and debate over the boundary between myth and history continues today.

Some historians, meanwhile, propose that Emtis may have been a real person.

Since ancient times, the Laclot have treated the transmission of knowledge as their mission and developed by collecting and preserving histories, magical texts, and documents from across the world. Even amid the rise and fall of nations and ages of war, they placed the survival of vanishing knowledge above all else and protected countless books and historical materials.

Their neutrality and exceptional learning gradually earned recognition from nations throughout the world, which concluded nonaggression treaties with the Independent City of Rezul. Scholars and researchers from across the world subsequently gathered there, and today it is widely known as the world's foremost city of scholarship.

The Laclot continue to pursue and preserve knowledge, passing the world's history and wisdom into the future through the restoration of ancient documents, the preservation of historical materials, and the investigation of ancient ruins.

Relations with Other Races

The Laclot favor no particular nation or race and maintain neutral relations with all peoples.

Nations throughout the world value their exceptional learning and impartiality and often ask them to authenticate historical materials, restore ancient documents, and investigate ancient ruins.

Scholars and researchers of every race also gather in the Independent City of Rezul, sharing knowledge and pursuing research together. The Laclot are therefore widely known as “guardians of knowledge” and “the people of wisdom” and are respected by many races.

They are never tolerant of those who exploit knowledge for selfish ends or destroy and plunder historically valuable artifacts and documents. They take a resolute stand against any individual or nation that threatens their mission to protect knowledge.

Notes

  • Although Rezul is known as the world's foremost city of scholarship, it is also affectionately called the “Land of Sweets.” Most Laclot have a sweet tooth, and seeing them snack on sweets while conducting research or copying manuscripts is part of everyday life.
  • A particular favorite is Sasselom, a black molasses with a rich sweetness and fragrance. It is used in sweets and drinks, and many also spread it directly on bread.
  • Research into why the Laclot enjoy sweets so much has continued since ancient times, but no clear conclusion has yet been reached.
