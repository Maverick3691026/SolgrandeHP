Oni

A minority people distinguished by horns and exceptionally powerful bodies.

Overview

The oni are known as a minority people in Solgrande. They are distinguished by horns growing from the forehead and powerful bodies that appear rigorously trained.

Their physical abilities surpass those of other races, and many use that strength to work as mercenaries or adventurers. Because so many choose dangerous lives, however, few live out their full natural lifespan.

Physical Traits

Oni have one or two horns on the forehead and are born with exceptional physical abilities. Even ordinary daily life tends to keep them in excellent condition, and both men and women are often well proportioned.

Culture and Daily Life

Many oni use their exceptional physical ability to earn a living as mercenaries or adventurers. They possess a strong instinct to preserve their people and often pursue romance and marriage actively, so families formed with other races are not uncommon.

Social Systems

Distinct rules and customs are preserved in the oni homeland. Many oni, however, live as mercenaries or adventurers in other nations and follow the laws and systems of the region or nation in which they reside.

Abilities and Aptitudes

Oni possess exceptionally high physical ability, strong aptitude for warrior and front-line roles, and outstanding strength and endurance.

History

A tradition preserved in the oni homeland holds that Nirham, god of battle, was the first and last pure-blooded oni. Nirham later had a child with a human, and the passing of that bloodline through generations is said to have given rise to the oni of today.

This account survives not only through oral tradition but also on ancient stone tablets in the oni homeland, and many oni preserve it as the story of their origin.

Relations with Other Races

Oni generally interact with other races, and marriages and families formed across racial lines are not unusual. Their physical ability earns them trust as mercenaries and adventurers, though their appearance alone can sometimes inspire fear.

Notes

The oni are a minority people in Solgrande. Oni women also tend to maintain strong, attractive physiques. The oni have an exceptionally powerful instinct to preserve their race, and both men and women tend to possess a strong sex drive linked to the urge to leave descendants. Many therefore pursue romance and marriage actively, and children with other races are not uncommon.
