Overview

Dwarves are a long-lived race with deep knowledge of ores and metalworking. Most dwarves living throughout the world are said to trace their ancestry to the mining city of Kazandor, and they excel at mining, smelting, and forging.

The weapons and armor they forge are of exceptional quality and have earned the firm trust of many knights and adventurers.

Physical Traits

Though short, dwarves have extremely sturdy builds. Even the tallest stand only about 150 centimeters. They have a high tolerance for pain, exceptional endurance, and a remarkable resistance to alcohol.

Culture and Daily Life

Dwarves live around mines and forges and excel at extracting and smelting ore and crafting weapons, armor, and tools. They possess a strong artisan spirit and take great pride in their skill and work. Smithing is more than a livelihood: it is a proud culture inherited from their ancestors and passed down through generations.

Social Systems

The mining city of Kazandor is governed by a council of twelve Elder Dwarves. The council oversees the city, manages its mines, preserves technical traditions, conducts diplomacy with other nations, and decides other important matters in dwarven society.

Abilities and Aptitudes

Dwarves excel at mining, smelting, and forging. They are skilled with heavy equipment and use their exceptional endurance to serve as shield bearers and front-line fighters.

History

Dwarves regard Marutero, the founder of smithing and first Dwarf King, as their ancestor. The era in which he lived and the circumstances surrounding the founding of their nation are the subject of many theories, and the details remain unknown.

Relations with Other Races

Dwarves generally interact with other races and supply nations with weapons and armor they have forged. Differences in values sometimes bring them into conflict with elves, who live alongside the forests, but not all are hostile, and many acknowledge and respect one another's knowledge and skill.

Notes

Most dwarves throughout the world trace their ancestry to the mining city of Kazandor. They are known as prodigious drinkers, and many have a high resistance to alcohol. In addition to weapons and armor, they excel at crafting tools and mechanical devices. Dwarven is a distinct language preserved through their artisan culture.
