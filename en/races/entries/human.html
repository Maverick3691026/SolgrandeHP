Human

The most populous race in Elamnova, distinguished by its diversity and adaptability.

Overview

Humans are the most populous race in Elamnova.

Few possess the exceptional innate gifts seen among some other races, but their strong environmental adaptability allows them to excel in martial arts, magic, commerce, farming, crafts, and virtually every other field.

Their desire to accomplish much within a short life sometimes leads long-lived races to call them the “short-lived race.” Their ability to change their way of life with their environment and era has also earned them the name “adaptive race.”

Heroes, kings, sages, merchants, artisans, adventurers, and ordinary people alike reflect the breadth of human talent and ways of life.

Physical Traits

Humans have few striking physical traits compared with other races. Physique, appearance, and ability vary greatly between individuals. Their adaptability enables them to live in many different lands.

Culture and Daily Life

Humans build societies through farming, commerce, crafts, scholarship, martial skill, magic, and many other pursuits. Culture and lifestyle differ greatly by nation and region, and this diversity is their defining characteristic.

Social Systems

Political structures, laws, and class systems differ from nation to nation. Human communities also exist in a wide range of environments, including extreme cold, deserts, and high mountains.

Abilities and Aptitudes

Humans possess strong environmental adaptability and can adjust flexibly to martial arts, magic, commerce, crafts, and many other fields. Many develop a broad range of abilities rather than specializing in a single talent.

History

Human history is extremely old. Tradition holds that the gods created humans first in their own image, but no reliable records or relics support this claim, and it is now treated as one of many myths.

Relations with Other Races

Humans generally interact actively and amicably with other races. Trade and cultural exchange are common, and many nations are home to several races living together.

Notes

Humans are the most populous race in Elamnova and are sometimes called the “short-lived race” or the “adaptive race.” Diversity and adaptability are their greatest strengths.
