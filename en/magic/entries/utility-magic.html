Utility Magic

Basic magic that makes daily life more convenient through lighting, ignition, cleaning, drying, and similar effects.

Overview

A collective term for magic that makes everyday life more convenient. Rather than combat magic, it includes basic spells used in daily life for lighting, ignition, cleaning, drying, and similar tasks.

Effects

It consumes the user's mana to create various phenomena that assist daily life. Each effect is small, but the magic is widespread and considered indispensable to everyday living.

Characteristics

Mana consumption is extremely low, and anyone with magical aptitude can learn it through brief training. It requires neither advanced incantations nor complex formulas and activates simply by channeling mana, making it common for starting fires, drying, washing, and other household assistance.

Mana Consumption

Minimal, equivalent to the energy an average adult expends by walking 15 lieves. Repeated use in daily life therefore causes almost no fatigue.

Learning Difficulty

Those with magical aptitude can learn it from childhood. Even the flame used to start a fire is no more than a spark, making it safe enough that many learn it as children.

Examples of Use

It is used to light campfires, cooking fires, and fireplaces, and to clean clothing, tableware, and the body. It also assists with drying laundry, ventilation, adjusting furnace heat, and many other everyday tasks.
