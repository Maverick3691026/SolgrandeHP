Arvhel Lioncourt

The story's protagonist, driven by curiosity and a hunger for knowledge to explore the world beyond his books.

Personality

Arvhel is shy, reserved, and slow to approach others, though he can converse when spoken to. Around trusted friends and family he becomes cheerful and playful.

His curiosity and appetite for knowledge are immense. He loves reading and can remain absorbed in a book for hours. He is kind, attentive, and willing to help at home, though he can be cold toward those he has come to dislike.

Because he takes others at their word, he is easily deceived and has poor judgment of character. He is lonely by nature yet also enjoys quiet time alone.

Individual Traits

Animals and small monsters are naturally drawn to him, and he treats living things gently. He has a sharp sense of smell and deft fingers, learned household magic early, and favors magic over swordsmanship.

He is poor at games of chance but strangely fortunate in dangerous situations. Stress easily upsets his digestion, though his stomach can otherwise handle almost any food.

Specialties

Cooking

He can prepare everyday meals and is especially good at recreating the taste of home cooking after trying it once.

Accessory Making

His dexterity allows him to fashion simple accessories and small objects from familiar materials.

Handling Animals and Monsters

Animals and small monsters rarely fear him and often grow attached to him.

Abilities and Skills

Arvhel is talented in magic and mastered household magic earlier than his peers. Kieval also taught him swordsmanship and close combat, but his aptitude leans toward magic. His physical ability is above average, though below that of a specialist warrior.

Appearance

Arvhel has refined features, moderate height, and a balanced, well-trained build without conspicuous bulk. He pays little attention to his hair and often leaves bedhead untouched.

He dislikes ornate clothing and favors practical, inconspicuous garments, nearly always choosing black.

Background

Arvhel was born as the eldest child of Kieval, a former soldier, and Sallis, a descendant of a mage. His sister Milica is one year younger.

He developed a love of books as soon as he learned to read, following each new question into another volume. At the same time, he grew up studying and training beside Tora, his closest friend and fellow villager.

Kieval taught him swordsmanship and hand-to-hand combat, but Arvhel's abundant, concentrated mana allowed him to master magic more quickly.

As a child he repeatedly brought home stray monsters because he could not abandon creatures separated from their groups, often involving his parents and even the neighborhood in the resulting commotion.

As he grew, his curiosity turned toward lands and creatures beyond Finridge. Books were no longer enough: he wanted to see the world himself. At fifteen he registered with the Adventurers' Guild and stepped into the world he had previously known only through reading.
