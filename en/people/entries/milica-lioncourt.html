Milica Lioncourt

An energetic, fiercely competitive girl with exceptional athletic ability and a natural talent for swordsmanship.

Personality

Milica is energetic and tomboyish and dislikes sitting still. Fiercely competitive, she keeps trying after failure or defeat rather than becoming discouraged.

She dislikes studying but is caring and especially gentle with those weaker than herself. She looks after small monsters Arvhel brings home as well as stray dogs and cats.

Individual Traits

Exceptional Athletic Ability

Milica runs, jumps, and climbs with ease. A childhood spent playing outdoors has made her agile and quick to learn physical movements.

Specialties

Tree Climbing

She uses her athletic ability to climb even tall trees without hesitation.

Housework

Regularly helping her mother Sallis has made her capable at household tasks, which she often volunteers to perform.

Abilities and Skills

Talent for Swordsmanship

Milica has shown great sword talent since childhood. Her body control allows her to convert instruction into movement quickly, and she steadily develops under Kieval's guidance despite her limited experience.

Instinctive Learning

She struggles with abstract explanations but excels at learning through observation and action. In swordsmanship, seeing a demonstration once is often more useful to her than a detailed verbal explanation.

Appearance

Milica wears her long light-brown hair in twin braids. Her large eyes and lively smile reflect her age and energetic nature.

She has a slender but healthy build and moves lightly. Even with a sword in hand she shows no fear, openly displaying her spirited, competitive personality.

Background

Milica was born in Finridge as the eldest daughter of Kieval and Sallis and is the younger sister of Arvhel Lioncourt.

She has been an active tomboy since childhood, often returning from outdoor play with injuries, yet she has always been especially kind to younger children and small animals.

She and Arvhel learned swordsmanship from Kieval. Her athleticism and natural talent have already made her the more skillful pure swordsman of the two, despite having no real combat experience.

Her competitive spirit will not let her remain silent when a friend is hurt; she has even punished boys who made her friends cry. In talent, athleticism, and temperament alike, she strongly resembles her father.
