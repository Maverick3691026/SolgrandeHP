Barks Harland

The captain of the Finridge village watch, protecting the community with calm judgment and extensive wilderness experience.

Occupation / Role
Captain of the Finridge village watch

Personality

Barks is normally calm and easygoing. He has a strong sense of responsibility and sees anything he accepts through to the end, regardless of his position.

He is not quick-tempered and remains composed in emergencies, judging situations accurately. Though strict about rules and discipline, he believes they are necessary to protect people's safety.

He allows children to try what they want and will not stop them from carrying weapons or learning swordsmanship if that is their wish. He expects them to take responsibility for things they chose and decided to pursue, but does not force them to continue something they dislike or find painful.

He rarely praises his son Tora openly, though he is privately delighted by the boy's growth and accomplishments.

Individual Traits

Barks is a great lover of alcohol and an exceptionally strong drinker. He regularly drinks with Kieval at the Hearthroot tavern and inn and can hold his liquor better than Kieval.

He looks forward to sampling drinks from different regions and is the first to search for rare varieties whenever a traveling merchant visits the village.

He is completely inept at cooking and household chores in general. At home, however, he is a dependable husband and father whom his wife Litt and the rest of the family rely upon.

Specialties

Liquor Tasting

He is skilled at distinguishing differences in the flavor and aroma of alcoholic drinks.

Fishing

He often goes fishing with his son Tora. Their skill is evenly matched, and each wins and loses in turn.

Abilities and Skills

Archery

Barks has handled bows skillfully since childhood and used one as his primary weapon during his adventuring years. He excels at attacking enemies from a distance.

Detection and Reconnaissance

He excels at surveying his surroundings, detecting enemies, and recognizing danger in advance. As an adventurer, he handled detection and reconnaissance for his parties.

Tracking

He is skilled at following targets by interpreting the traces they leave behind.

Wilderness Skills

His experience as an adventurer has made him highly skilled at camping, survival, and other forms of wilderness activity.

Appearance

Barks is a ruggedly handsome man distinguished by short, neatly trimmed blond hair and stubble. His solid build and well-trained body reflect many years of practical combat experience.

Background

Barks was born into a farming family in Finridge Village.

He and Kieval were close from childhood, and together they were a pair of uncontrollable troublemakers. When Kieval entered the army of the Principality of Bermouthia at age fourteen, Barks mended his ways and became an adventurer at fifteen.

Using the bow he had mastered since childhood, he served in various parties as a specialist in detection, reconnaissance, tracking, and ranged attacks, eventually reaching C rank.

During his adventuring career, he began a relationship with Litt, who belonged to the same party.

Later, when Barks was preparing for his B-rank promotion examination, Kieval retired from the army and returned to Finridge after Sallis became pregnant. Barks retired from adventuring at the same time and married Litt.

After returning home, he joined the Finridge village watch and applied the experience he had gained as an adventurer. His many accomplishments eventually earned him appointment as its captain.

He and Litt have been blessed with two children, and he now lives with his family while protecting the village as captain of the watch.

Notes

When Barks obtains a rare drink, he wants to keep it all for himself, and he sometimes quarrels with Kieval when Kieval drinks it without permission.

He is skilled with his hands and better at detailed work than Kieval.

He is also good at physical labor.
