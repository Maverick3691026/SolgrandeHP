Céline Haldan

A healer who runs Finridge Village's clinic and supports its people through her knowledge of herbs and medicine.

Personality

Céline is gentle, considerate, and caring. Her calm smile and kindness have made her beloved as the “mother of the village.” Children naturally gather at her clinic, and she is the first to help whenever someone is injured or ill.

Her knowledge of medicine and herbs allows her to teach local children as well as treat patients. When her husband Ruben must make a difficult decision as village chief, Céline helps ease the villagers' hearts.

Individual Traits

She always serves warm herbal tea to anyone who comes seeking advice. She rarely becomes angry, but quietly admonishes anyone who behaves recklessly.

Specialties

Céline excels at identifying, combining, and preparing medicinal herbs. She is skilled in first aid and treatment, notices subtle changes in a person's health, and has turned the clinic into a small place of learning for village children.

Abilities and Skills

Pharmacology

She can prepare basic remedies such as salves and fever medicine and has especially deep knowledge of herbs native to the Finridge area.

Alchemy

Her alchemical skill ranges from basic to intermediate, with a focus on medicines and therapeutic preparations made from herbs and minerals.

Healing

Years of treating villagers have made her highly capable in wound care and emergency responses to illness.

Herb Cultivation

She grows medicinal plants around the clinic to maintain her own supply of ingredients.

Appearance

Céline is a composed woman with long, silver-white hair worn loose, refined features, and a gentle gaze. Her soft smile and calm manner give even strangers a sense of reassurance.

She wears clothing in subdued colors and a necklace with a small moon-shaped ornament. Her quiet warmth and intelligence make her a comforting presence to both children and adults who visit the clinic.

Background

Céline was born and raised in Dolgaf Village, a settlement neighboring Finridge. Her parents were former adventurers from the same party who opened a weapons shop after retiring. As a child she was spirited and tomboyish, often returning home with dirty clothes and injuries after confronting bullies.

As she matured, her natural compassion inspired an interest in herbs and healing. She traveled to Tsadar to study pharmacology and alchemy, where she learned of Finridge and its rich medicinal plants.

After returning home, she continued her research while helping at her parents' shop. Her beauty and grace earned her renown as the finest beauty in Dolgaf and brought many proposals.

She eventually met Ruben, a Finridge blacksmith seven years her senior, when he visited the shop to trade weapons. His sincerity and stories of Finridge drew her to both him and the village where her knowledge could truly help others.

After their marriage she moved to Finridge, opened its clinic, and supported Ruben as village chief. Today her pharmacological and alchemical knowledge sustains the villagers' health, and people of every generation cherish her as the “mother of the village.”
