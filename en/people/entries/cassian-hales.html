    Personality

    Cassian is serious and diligent. He faithfully continues his daily prayers, training, reading, and lessons for the village children.

    He rarely abandons his routine because of the weather or his physical condition, and earns the trust of those around him through his self-discipline.

    Characteristics

    Beneath his priestly robes, he has a lean and well-trained build. Although he has no formal combat training, he exercises every day for his health and self-discipline.

    He readily volunteers for strenuous work, including carrying heavy loads and chopping firewood. The villagers affectionately joke that he is “a priest unlike a priest.”

    Talents

    Cassian excels at physical work such as chopping firewood and at painting. Painting is a particular hobby of his, and the church walls display many of his depictions of myths, saints, and landscapes from each season.

    His paintings of Finridge's peaceful daily life and beautiful natural surroundings are cherished by the villagers, and travelers sometimes take one home as a memento of their journey.

    He does not sell his paintings and gives them freely to villagers and travelers who ask for one.

    Abilities and Skills

    Basic healing magic, antidote magic, pharmacology, and purification magic (Turn Undead).

    Appearance

    Cassian has soft brown hair and gentle brown eyes. He keeps a neatly trimmed short beard and is rarely without a calm smile.

    He wears clean, modest priestly robes and carries sacred scripture and prayer beads. Though gentle in appearance, his bearing reveals the inner strength built through daily training.

    Background

    Cassian was born to a married couple who served as priests at the church in Finridge Village. Raised in the church, he grew up under the watchful care of many villagers.

    Darius, son of village chief Ruben, has been his childhood friend since they grew up together, and the two still share deep mutual trust.

    In his youth, Cassian traveled to the Temple of Karad Valdea, where he studied theology, healing magic, pharmacology, and purification magic.

    At the temple he also learned exorcism to protect people from undead and evil spirits. He did not excel in swordsmanship or combat technique, however, and was never comfortable fighting with a weapon himself.

    After completing his training, he returned to Finridge Village and succeeded his parents as the church's priest.

    He now conducts worship services, weddings, funerals, and other ceremonies while teaching children reading, writing, and arithmetic three mornings each week, when few worshippers are present.

    He also shelters two children orphaned under different circumstances and raises them with support from the villagers. Donations and ceremonial honoraria are used to maintain the church and support the orphans, while Cassian himself continues to live modestly.

    Though normally gentle and averse to conflict, he is prepared to stand at the forefront and protect the village with purification magic when undead or evil spirits threaten human lives.
