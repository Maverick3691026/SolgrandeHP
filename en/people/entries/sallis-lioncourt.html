A kind-hearted woman raised in a magic-item shop in Fulais who treats others with quiet warmth.

Personality

Sallis is gentle and considerate, treating everyone with equal kindness. She listens until others have finished speaking and cannot ignore someone in need.

Though usually reserved, she has the courage to place herself in danger when protecting her family.

Individual Traits

She can become slightly overprotective where her family is concerned.

Brewing herbal tea is part of her daily routine, and she always welcomes visitors with a warm drink. Even on busy days she tends the flowers in her garden, and her peaceful way of life naturally puts visitors at ease.

Appearance

Sallis is a graceful woman with long, glossy chestnut hair and soft amber eyes. Her gentle smile gives even strangers a sense of reassurance.

She favors subdued clothing over flamboyant dress. Years of housework and helping in the shop have given her light, capable movements and an unadorned beauty.

Although renowned in town for her beauty, she pays it no mind and treats everyone naturally. Her grandmother often says that she strongly resembles her great-great-grandmother, Elena Krone.

Background

Sallis was born and raised in Fulais, a town of roughly 3,000 people in southeastern Sinestadia near the borders with the Principality of Bermouthia and the Mining City of Kazandor.

The Krone family has operated a magic-item shop in Fulais for generations. Her great-great-grandmother Elena Krone was a legendary mage known in the Kingdom of Trinesia as “The Witch's Crown.” Elena's grimoires and magical tools have been carefully passed down through the shop, where Sallis's father protects them while running the business.

Military posts and checkpoints made conflict a familiar presence during her childhood. Seeing soldiers wounded on border duty taught her the value of life and the sorrow of conflict, shaping her gentle and compassionate nature.

She and her brother learned magic from their grandmother from an early age, and Sallis mastered household magic and beginner healing magic. Though talented, she chose not to pursue formal training and instead helped her parents in the shop while valuing everyday connections with others. Her brother, meanwhile, developed his talent under their grandmother and later became a teacher at the royal capital's academy of magic.

Fulais is surrounded by rich forests and grasslands, and patrols from both nations regularly guarded the border. Residents commonly gathered wild plants and medicinal herbs.

One day, Sallis encountered a monster while gathering herbs in the forest. She fled as far as the Bermouthian border, where the Bermouthian army responded to the disturbance.

Kieval Lioncourt, then sixteen and commander of the Sinestadian-front defense force, defeated the monster and protected Sallis as a civilian despite her belonging to an enemy nation. The encounter drew them together, and they later married.
