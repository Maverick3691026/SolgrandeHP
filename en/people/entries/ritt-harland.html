Ritt Harland

A warmhearted former adventurer whose bold, easygoing spirit holds her family together.

Personality

Ritt is a bold, broad-minded mother who does not concern herself with trifles.

Bright and spirited, she remains unfazed by minor injuries or failures.

On the other hand, she shows no mercy when scolding her son Tora for misbehaving.

She loves her family deeply. Though worry sometimes drives her to shout, she is warmhearted and caring at her core.

Specialties

Camp Cooking

Ritt excels at the camp cooking skills she developed during her adventuring years.

She expertly controls a campfire and can efficiently prepare grilled meat and fish, stews in a large pot, and other meals in the wilderness.

She makes good use of limited ingredients and seasonings, producing hearty food even with simple equipment.

She is especially skilled with meat dishes. Her methods may be vigorous, but her seasoning is precise, and during her adventuring days she kept her companions well fed.

Abilities and Skills

Heavy Blow

A combat technique that combines her trained arm strength and body weight into a powerful strike. It excels at breaking an opponent's guard, and she used it to great effect as a frontline warrior during her adventuring years.

Leg Sweep

A combat technique in which she reads an opponent's balance and footwork, then sweeps their feet out from under them. It is useful for disrupting enemy attacks and creating openings for her allies.

Appearance

Ritt wears her reddish-brown hair tied back, with her bangs parted to either side.

She has green eyes and a healthy, rosy complexion. Her broad shoulders and well-toned build reflect her past as an adventurer.

She usually wears a white blouse, a brown vest, and a long skirt, with an apron tied around her waist.

Background

Ritt was born in Finridge Village.

She grew up there alongside her childhood friends Barks Harland and Kieval Lioncourt.

After Kieval entered the army of the Principality of Bermouthia at age fourteen and Barks became an adventurer at fifteen, Ritt also began working as an adventurer.

She served on the front line as a warrior and used her natural strength and stamina to carry equipment and supplies as well.

During her adventuring career, she began a relationship with Barks, who belonged to the same party.

Later, Sallis's pregnancy led Kieval to retire from the army and return home. Barks retired from adventuring at the same time and married Ritt.

After their marriage, they built a home in Finridge Village and were blessed with two children, their son Tora and a daughter. Ritt now cultivates medicinal herbs alongside her housework and childcare.

She is constantly kept busy by the mischievous Tora and loudly scolds him whenever he gets into trouble.

Notes

Ritt loves both of her children deeply, and the strength of that love makes her extremely strict about discipline.

Although minor injuries and failures do not trouble her, she comes down hard on dangerous pranks and behavior that causes problems for others.

Her voice is especially loud when she scolds the mischievous Tora and can be heard throughout the neighborhood.
