Pedra

A Coyote Beastfolk adventurer who uses her keen senses and self-taught combat style to complete assignments across the land.

Personality

Quiet, faithful to her assignments, and highly responsible.

Pedra speaks little and avoids revealing more about herself than necessary. She is devoted to every assignment she accepts, and her strong sense of responsibility drives her to see each one through to the end.

She will not hesitate to place herself in danger to protect those entrusted to her care.

Specialty

Survival Skills

Having endured harsh conditions since childhood, Pedra is adept at camping, securing food, and surviving with minimal supplies.

Abilities and Skills

Exceptional Hearing

Pedra possesses the acute hearing characteristic of Coyote Beastfolk. She excels at detecting faint sounds and signs of nearby prey.

Exceptional Sense of Smell

She can detect prey and determine its direction of travel by scent, an ability she uses for hunting and tracking.

High Acceleration

The physical abilities of Coyote Beastfolk allow her to accelerate from a standstill to 1.9 Veld in an instant. While hunting, she uses this burst of speed to close the distance to her prey at once.

Self-Taught Combat Style

Pedra has never received formal combat training. She developed her fighting style through the hunts she undertook from childhood to survive, combining her hearing, sense of smell, acceleration, and other racial traits into an approach of her own.

History

Pedra's birthplace is unknown. She lived in the slums of the Kingdom of Svarn, where food was scarce throughout her childhood.

She survived from day to day by scavenging scraps and other discarded food to stave off hunger.

After suffering persecution between races, she fled the Kingdom of Svarn and migrated to the Principality of Bermouthia.

There, she used the exceptional hearing, sense of smell, and acceleration characteristic of Coyote Beastfolk to hunt low-rank monsters such as Fluffers. She sold their materials to cover her living expenses while steadily saving money.

With those savings, Pedra eventually registered with the Adventurers' Guild. She has never formally studied combat; instead, she relies on the self-taught techniques she developed through the hunts that kept her alive since childhood.

She now travels from place to place as an E-rank adventurer, earning her living through escort work, monster-hunting assignments, and similar requests.
