Rokel Kolpor

An amiable and loyal merchant who runs a general store in Ug-Nel.

Personality

Rokel is friendly and approachable, and quickly feels at ease even with people he has just met.

He is loyal and makes a point of repaying every kindness shown to him.

He is especially grateful to Arvhel and Tora for saving his party, generously allowing each of them to choose an item from his merchandise as thanks. At the same time, he is practical and steady: rather than taking reckless risks for a large profit, he prefers to keep his business growing within his means.

Traits

Because of the poverty he experienced as a child, Rokel rarely throws anything away and uses every serviceable item for as long as possible.

When something suffers minor damage, he repairs it himself instead of replacing it. This habit has made him dexterous enough to mend simple tools and household goods.

Specialty

Mental Arithmetic

He calculates extremely quickly, handling sales, change, and purchase prices in his head.

The proprietor of the tavern where he once worked taught him arithmetic, and years of trade sharpened the skill further.

Abilities and Skills

Foresight

Rokel has excellent commercial foresight.

By observing local conditions and changes in the market, he can anticipate which goods will soon be needed.

He notices shortages and rising demand early, securing suppliers before the need becomes pressing and conducting business with an eye to what lies ahead.

History

Rokel was born in Ug-Nel in the Principality of Bermouthia.

He grew up in a poor household and spent much of his childhood hungry, unable to eat his fill.

From an early age, he dreamed of one day living without hunger.

When he came of age, he began working as a server at an Ug-Nel tavern, where the proprietor taught him reading, writing, and arithmetic.

He saved money little by little while working, with the goal of opening a shop of his own.

To raise more capital, he bought a weapon with his savings and hunted weak monsters such as Fluffers between shifts. He never chased a single great windfall; instead, he sold hides, magic stones, and other materials, steadily combining those earnings with his wages until he had enough to start a business.

While working at the tavern, he met a woman who was also employed there as a server. They grew close and eventually fell in love. She understood his dream of owning a shop and supported his efforts wholeheartedly.

Using the money he had saved over many years, Rokel renovated his home in Ug-Nel and finally opened the general store he had long dreamed of.

He later married the woman who had supported him for so long.

Today, he runs the store while raising their only son, Komel. His wife manages the shop whenever Rokel leaves to purchase stock, and Komel sometimes accompanies his father to gain experience as a merchant.

Notes

Rokel's wife manages the general store whenever he is away from Ug-Nel purchasing stock.

He sometimes brings Komel along on buying trips so his son can gain experience as a merchant.

After Arvhel and Tora saved him near Finridge Village, Rokel formed ties with the Lioncourt and Harland families.
