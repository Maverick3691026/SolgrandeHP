Marta Brown

The proprietress of the Hearthroot tavern and inn, welcoming guests with warm food and her friendly nature.

Personality

Marta is cheerful, friendly, and approachable, treating everyone with the same easy warmth.

She is caring and attentive, always looking out for others, especially children and familiar customers.

Traits

Marta is naturally caring and cannot help fussing over regular customers and children.

Nothing makes her happier than seeing people enjoy the food she has prepared. She sometimes gives growing children larger portions without even being asked.

Her many years working at a tavern and inn have made her highly attentive to everything happening around the room. Even while speaking with a customer, she readily notices empty plates and cups or a guest in need of help.

She is also accustomed to dealing with intoxicated patrons and remains unfazed by ordinary drunken behavior.

She treats first-time travelers with such natural friendliness that they soon feel like old acquaintances, and many guests return to Hearthroot.

Specialty

Remembering Faces and Names

Marta is skilled at remembering people's faces and names. Once she has served someone, she often remembers them even after a long absence.

Abilities and Skills

Cooking

Having helped at Hearthroot since childhood, Marta is an accomplished cook who can quickly prepare a meal even from limited ingredients.

History

Marta was born in Finridge Village and grew up in the Hearthroot tavern and inn from an early age.

Her family had operated Hearthroot since before she was born. By helping her parents from childhood, she naturally learned cooking, customer service, and the other work involved in running a tavern and inn.

Even as a young woman, she was friendly and welcoming, treating visiting travelers with the same warmth she showed the villagers.

Eventually, she met a man who stopped at Hearthroot during his travels.

He took a liking to the inn's peaceful atmosphere and food and began visiting Hearthroot whenever his journeys brought him through Finridge.

As they met time and again, the two grew close, fell in love, and married. Her husband settled in Finridge and began helping Marta support Hearthroot.

They later had a daughter, Emma, and Marta eventually inherited Hearthroot from her parents and became its proprietress.

She continues to run Hearthroot with her family, welcoming villagers and travelers with the same warm atmosphere and cooking the inn has long been known for.

Notes

Marta's husband purchases the alcohol, food, and other supplies used at Hearthroot and is often away from Finridge.

As a result, Marta and Emma usually serve the customers. The two are beloved by villagers and travelers as the faces of Hearthroot.
