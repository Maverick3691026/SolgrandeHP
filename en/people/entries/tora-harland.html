Tora Harland

A cheerful, easygoing boy with remarkable sword aptitude who trains toward his dream of becoming a knight.

Personality

Tora is cheerful, broad-minded, and unconcerned with details. He speaks and acts directly, dislikes intrigue, and struggles with academic study.

He values his companions and will put himself in danger to protect them, even remaining behind so others can escape. With a sword in hand, his usual levity vanishes and he becomes intensely focused and diligent.

Individual Traits

Exceptional Sword Aptitude

Tora has displayed remarkable talent with a sword since childhood. Under Kieval's instruction and through independent practice, he has learned to exploit openings with skill beyond his years.

Tireless Stamina

His extraordinary endurance allows him to continue physical activity and sword practice long after those around him have tired.

Specialty

Fishing

Tora has loved fishing since childhood. He understands fish behavior and reads currents and surroundings to locate them, often bringing his catch home for his family.

Abilities and Skills

Preemptive Reading

He reads an opponent's stance, gaze, and movement to act one step ahead, a core ability that improves with experience.

Counter Initiative

He deliberately presents an opening, invites an attack, reads the resulting motion, and counters from a favorable position.

Appearance

Tora is an energetic boy with casually arranged short brown hair, a bright expression, and a lean, well-conditioned body built through constant sword practice.

Background

Tora was born in Finridge. His father, Barks Harland, commands the village watch, while his mother, Litt, cultivates medicinal herbs. He is also a caring older brother to a sister six years younger.

An active and mischievous child, he often played with sticks as swords and returned home injured. He became inseparable from his neighbor Arvhel and began learning swordsmanship from Arvhel's father Kieval.

Natural aptitude, Kieval's guidance, and relentless practice steadily improved his skill. By age ten, he and Arvhel could hunt low-rank monsters.

Tora admires the knights active in the capital and dreams of becoming one himself. He continues training every day to achieve that goal.
