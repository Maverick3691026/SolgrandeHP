Darius Haldan

A former adventurer and shrewd appraiser who supports daily life in Finridge through his general store.

Personality

Darius is caring, easygoing, and friendly. He treats villagers equally regardless of status or age and speaks casually even with children, who know him as an approachable figure. If someone asks for a favor that requires only a little effort, he readily agrees.

Although usually unconcerned with minor details, he is practical and astute in business. Years of experience have given him a keen eye for appraisal and a strong knowledge of market prices, allowing him to judge an item's value calmly.

He is skilled at negotiating prices, but dislikes haggling someone down through argument. Instead, he excels at finding terms both sides can accept.

His experience as a former adventurer keeps him composed in the face of moderate danger or unexpected events. He is not reckless, however, and remains a sensible man who observes proper conduct when it matters.

Individual Traits

Having handled many kinds of weapons and tools since childhood, Darius instinctively examines the construction and materials of any rare or unfamiliar item he encounters. He also enjoys commercial bargaining, taking pleasure in the exchange itself.

Specialty

Price negotiation

Abilities and Skills

Physical enhancement and appraisal

Background

Darius was born in Finridge Village as the son of Ruben and Céline. From childhood, Céline taught him basic apothecary knowledge and potion preparation, while Ruben taught him how to handle weapons and practice smithing.

His early exposure to a wide variety of arms and tools also developed his sharp eye for judging the quality of goods.

He became an adventurer in his late teens and advanced as far as D rank. After traveling widely, he returned to his native Finridge with the money he had saved and opened a general store.

Until then, the village had depended on visiting merchants for many everyday necessities and tools. His shop has since become one of the businesses that support daily life in Finridge.

Darius is currently unmarried. Cassian is his close friend, and he often visits the church.
