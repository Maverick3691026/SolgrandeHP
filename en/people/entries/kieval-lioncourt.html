Kieval Lioncourt

A warm-hearted member of the Finridge village watch who protects the peaceful lives of his family and neighbors.

Personality

Kieval is emotional, compassionate, and unable to ignore anyone in trouble. He is stubborn and holds firmly to what he believes, favoring practical experience over difficult theories and books.

He is gentle with children, treats everyone equally, and dislikes conflict. Despite his martial skill, he says that avoiding a fight is always best.

Individual Traits

He is overprotective and somewhat anxious about his children and pets, yet extends the same care to the village's children. His sincerity has earned deep trust, and villagers turn to him first when problems arise.

Abilities and Skills

Swordsmanship and Hand-to-Hand Combat

Kieval is highly skilled with both the sword and his bare hands. In his youth he subdued several bandits without drawing a weapon, and his ability became widely known within Bermouthia's army.

Appearance

He has short, neatly kept hair and a powerful, muscular body forged through real combat. His sharp gaze and rugged features can frighten strangers, but those who know his gentle, caring nature hold him in great affection.

Background

Kieval was born in Finridge to parents who cultivated medicinal herbs. His extraordinary talent with the sword emerged in childhood, and he entered Bermouthia's army at the exceptional age of fourteen.

His accomplishments, combat ability, and calm judgment earned the trust of those around him. Within two years he was promoted to commander of the defense force facing Sinestadia.

During a mission he rescued Sallis from a monster. They fell in love, maintained their bond for eight years, and when Sallis became pregnant, Kieval retired from the army at age twenty-four.

He returned to Finridge, where he now serves in the village watch and cultivates the herbs inherited from his parents. Protecting his family's and neighbors' peaceful lives has become his most important duty.

Related People

Related Nations and Organizations

Notes

When Kieval was young, a monster killed his sister, who was two years younger. The loss left a deep wound and drove him to seek the strength to protect those he loves. It also shaped the exceptional gentleness he shows children and family.
