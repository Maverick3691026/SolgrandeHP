Ruben Haldan

The chief of Finridge Village and a veteran blacksmith who supports its daily life.

Personality

Ruben is calm and good-natured. He rarely raises his voice in anger and listens carefully to the villagers.

When a decision must be made, however, his strong sense of responsibility and resolve allow him to act without hesitation.

Individual Traits

He has earned the villagers' deep trust and possesses excellent powers of coordination and leadership. He is also skilled at mediating disputes and negotiating with outsiders.

Specialties

Mediation and winning people's trust.

He excels at settling disputes between villagers peacefully and uniting the village.

Abilities and Skills

Blacksmithing

Ruben works in the smithy attached to his home. He maintains weapons and armor for the village watch and repairs everything from hoes and sickles to cooking pots and knives. His smithy is indispensable to life in Finridge, and villagers often visit with tools to repair or matters to discuss.

Appearance

Years at the forge have left Ruben powerfully built despite his age, with broad shoulders and a sturdy frame. His hair and full beard are white.

His face has the dignity of a lifelong craftsman, while his arms and hands bear burns and scars from smithing. He usually wears a leather apron over his work clothes. He looks more like a master blacksmith than a village chief, and the villagers regard him as someone they can depend upon.

Background

Ruben was born into a family of blacksmiths in Finridge Village in the Principality of Bermouthia. He helped in his father's smithy from childhood and learned the foundations of the craft. An only child, he lost both parents while still young, and his early experience of loneliness taught him to value human bonds above all else.

As a young man, his father encouraged him to improve his craft in the mining city of Kazandor, an ally of the Granzar Forest Region. Carrying a letter of introduction to one of his father's old friends, Ruben studied advanced smithing under that craftsman.

While he was still training, word arrived that his father had died. Ruben returned home before completing his studies and resolved to inherit the smithy his father had left behind.

Using the skills he had acquired in Kazandor, he supported the villagers by maintaining the watch's arms and armor and forging or repairing farm tools and household goods. His honest work and good character earned such trust that the former chief recommended him as a successor, and the villagers supported his appointment as chief of Finridge.

He later met Céline, who had studied pharmacology and alchemy, while visiting Dolgaf to trade weapons. They grew close and married, and he welcomed her to Finridge. Together they have continued to support the village and its people.

Ruben still leads the village while remaining active as a blacksmith. He and Céline have a son, Darius, and a daughter, Camila, who married and established her own household in Finridge.
