Speed Notation

Speed Notation

Heiramnova's common method of expressing speed by the distance traveled within a fixed time.

Basic Information

Name
Method of Expressing Speed
Classification
Speed notation
Related Category
Units and measurement
Related Terms
Til, Riiv, Garna, Verd, and units of time

Definition

Heiramnova has no independent unit used only for speed. Speed is expressed as the distance traveled within a fixed time.

Normally, “per hour” is placed after a unit of length, or the notation “/h” is used.

Examples

30 Riiv per hour

30 Riiv/h

Both mean traveling 30 Riiv in one hour.

Units Used

Riiv, Garna, and Verd are used according to the speed and scale of travel.

Riiv/h
Fine movement speeds of people or small animals, and movement over short distances.
Garna/h
Everyday travel such as walking, wagons, mounted travel, and ships.
Verd/h
Very fast travel such as flying creatures, high-speed ships, and magical vehicles.

Til/h may be used when necessary, but is uncommon except for extremely slow movement or precise measurement.

Examples of Use

A person walking at a normal pace travels at approximately 50 Garna per hour.

A wagon generally travels at about 100 to 150 Garna per hour, depending on road conditions and its load.

A Letterwing reaches about 12 Verd per hour in flight, equivalent to roughly 380 km/h in real-world terms.

Choice of Notation

In prose, “XX per hour” is the standard form.

Example: A Letterwing reaches a maximum of approximately 12 Verd per hour.

In bestiaries, maps, survey records, Adventurers Guild documents, and other contexts requiring concise numerical notation, “XX/h” is used.

Example: Maximum flight speed: approximately 12 Verd/h

Notes

Speed is calculated by dividing distance traveled by elapsed time.

The same speed can be converted into different length units depending on the subject and purpose. To avoid excessively large numbers, Garna/h is generally used for everyday movement and Verd/h for high-speed movement.
