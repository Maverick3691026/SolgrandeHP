Heiramnova

Heiramnova

A planet with two moons, consisting of one continent and several island chains.

Basic Information

Name
Heiramnova
Classification
Planet
Orbital Period
360 days
Moons
Lumia and Sharmena

Overview

  • Its diameter and gravity are approximately the same as Earth's.
  • It consists of one continent and several island chains.
  • The equatorial region is warm and fertile, while colder regions exist in the north and south.
  • Its two moons affect the tides, calendar, and religious rites.

Celestial Bodies

The Sun

A bluish-white star that shines slightly more brightly than Earth's sun, casting clear light and shadow across the world.

Lumia

A silver moon with a 30-day orbital period. This cycle defines Heiramnova's 30-day month.

Sharmena

A reddish-copper moon with a 60-day orbital period.

The moons have a 2:1 orbital resonance: Sharmena completes one orbit while Lumia completes two. Their gravity influences tides, ocean currents, and magical flows, making them important to navigation, agriculture, astronomy, and religious rites.

The Annual Calendar

Heiramnova orbits its primary star in 360 days, defining one year.

1. Month of Dawn
The new year begins with festivals that light a new flame.
2. Month of Thinning Ice
The cold eases, river ice breaks, and spring approaches.
3. Month of Sprouting
Plants bud and farmers prepare their fields.
4. Month of Wind Song
Strong seasonal winds blow, and spirit-worshiping regions hold wind festivals.
5. Month of Sunlight
Sunlight strengthens and the days grow longer.
6. Month of Blazing Heat
The hottest season brings fire festivals and solstice rites.
7. Month of the Earth
Grain ripens and fields turn golden.
8. Month of Harvest
The harvest peaks, great markets open, and trade flourishes.
9. Month of the Hunt
Hunting for winter preparations begins in earnest.
10. Month of Silent Frost
The first frost arrives, and many rites honor the dead.
11. Shadow Month
The year's longest nights make this a busy month for astrologers.
12. Month of the Flameheart
Fire purifies the closing year, and Flameheart festivals welcome the new one.

Months and Weeks

A month has 30 days. A week consists of ten days named for attributes or roles.

  1. Flame Day (fire)
  2. Healing Day (restoration)
  3. Tide Day (water)
  4. Ice Day (ice)
  5. Wind Day (wind)
  6. Thunder Day (lightning)
  7. Earth Day (earth)
  8. Dark Day (darkness)
  9. Light Day (light)
  10. Rest Day (rest, no attribute)

Time

Heiramnova uses the same time units as Earth: 24 hours per day, 60 minutes per hour, and 60 seconds per minute.
