Fruits of Solgrande

Fruits of Heiramnova I

A guide to fruits found in Heiramnova, including their effects, uses, and flavors.

Basic Information

Classification
Plants and Fruits
Number Listed
14 varieties
Primary Uses
Food, medicine, processing, and fragrance

Nebralis

  • Vision support (night vision): slightly improves visibility in darkness.
  • Improved concentration: supports long work and stable spellcasting.
  • Minor mana recovery: restores mana gradually, though less than a mana potion.

Distributed even among nobles and high-ranking adventurers.

Mildly tart with a gentle sweet-and-sour flavor.

Razlis

  • Enhanced senses: heightens awareness of enemies and surrounding changes.
  • Improved reactions: slightly sharpens split-second judgment and evasion.
  • Mild stimulant: heightens combat focus briefly.

Favored by adventurers, scouts, and front-line fighters.

Fairly tart with faint sweetness in the finish.

Frelis

  • Minor stamina recovery: eases fatigue and provides light vitality.
  • Mental calm: relieves anxiety and tension.
  • Enhanced appetite: increases satisfaction from meals and encourages appetite.

Widely distributed from ordinary households to adventurers.

Strongly sweet with slight acidity for a familiar, balanced taste.

Junelis

  • Minor detoxification: eases mild poisons and bodily impurities.
  • Digestive support: regulates the stomach and reduces discomfort after meals.
  • Gentle mental calm: settles the mind and encourages clear judgment.

Favored by apothecaries, cooks, and long-distance merchants.

A refreshing aroma with slight bitterness and a spice-like finish.

Braclis

  • Gradual endurance enhancement: reduces fatigue during prolonged activity.
  • Minor defense enhancement: slightly improves physical resilience.
  • Improved circulation: promotes blood flow and recovery efficiency.

Favored by adventurers and mercenaries on long journeys or prolonged battles.

Subdued sweetness with deep body, slight astringency, and acidity.

Cranlis

  • Minor purification: encourages removal of waste and toxins.
  • Antibacterial effect: helps prevent mild infection and illness.
  • Sustained focus: keeps the mind clear during long activity.

Favored by travelers, healers, and adventurers on extended missions.

Strong acidity with a clean, refreshing finish.

Noxia

  • Strong vision support: reduces eye fatigue and sharpens vision.
  • Sustained minor-plus mana recovery: gradually restores mana.
  • Enhanced concentration: supports stable thought and lasting focus.

Favored by mages, researchers, and those working long hours.

Strongly tart with a rich, focused flavor.

Calva

  • Moderate vision support: steadies vision and slightly improves visibility.
  • Minor mana recovery: restores mana gently.
  • Mental calm: relieves tension and maintains stability.

An approachable fruit for ordinary adventurers and everyday use.

Slightly sweet, with mellow richness and mild acidity.

Ruvena

  • Minor circulation boost: improves blood flow and physical performance.
  • Sensory activation: slightly sharpens the senses and reactions.
  • Minor fatigue recovery: eases accumulated weariness.

Favored by front-line fighters, light warriors, and speed-focused adventurers.

Strong acidity and refreshing stimulation with a clean finish.

Guslis

  • Minor natural recovery enhancement: speeds recovery from wounds and fatigue.
  • Minor resistance enhancement: grants resistance to environmental change and mild poison.
  • Physical regulation: balances the body and reduces excessive strain.

Favored by rangers, gatherers, and wilderness workers.

Strongly tart with a rough, green flavor.

Suglan

  • Minor olfactory stimulation: gently awakens the mind and sharpens the senses.
  • Mild mental calm: its aroma eases tension and anxiety.
  • Concentration support: its fragrance helps organize thought.

Very low in nutrition and poorly suited for eating.

Its powerful aroma is used mainly in perfumes and scented oils.

Processing further enhances the aroma, making it a luxury product.

Slightly tart, though the aroma overwhelms the flavor.

Thornlis

  • Moderate stamina recovery: speeds fatigue recovery and maintains endurance.
  • Strong antioxidant effect: limits bodily damage and improves recovery.
  • Immune enhancement: improves resistance to illness and environmental stress.

Extremely nutritious and sometimes called the fruit of vitality.

Grows on thorny shrubs and is dangerous to harvest.

Strongly tart with slight bitterness and a rich, wild flavor.

Shadolis

  • Minor stamina recovery: natural sweetness gently relieves fatigue.
  • Mental calm: provides reassurance and eases tension.
  • Sustained recovery support: gently improves other recovery effects.

A rare fruit harvested only in cold regions.

Sweet and nearly free of harsh flavors; excellent eaten fresh.

Widely used in pies and jam because it processes well.

Gentle sweetness with a clean finish.

Kuromino Kagura

  • Strong stamina recovery: continuously promotes recovery after consumption.
  • Extremely high nutrition: among the most nutritious of all fruits.
  • Endurance enhancement: supports basic physical ability and prolonged activity.

Grows widely but is vulnerable to light and environmental competition, so distribution is scattered.

Difficult to find and requiring knowledge and experience to harvest.

Extremely sour and bitter, making it poorly suited for eating raw.

Processing reveals its value as premium preserved food or medicinal material.
