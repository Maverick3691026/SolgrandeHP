Viala

The common currency widely circulated throughout Elamnova.

Definition

Viala is the common currency widely circulated throughout Elamnova.

Its name comes from Rainvia, deity of trade, commerce, wealth, contracts, and journeys.

For merchants, it represents not only money but also legitimate exchange and proof of contract.

Viala is minted in many nations, but standards for weight, metal purity, and markings are unified and administered by the temples of Rainvia.

Details

The currency originated in the prayers merchants offered to Rainvia for safe trade and legitimate contracts. As trade routes developed, it spread across borders and became the common currency now used throughout Elamnova.

Viala does not belong to any single nation. Each nation's mint produces it according to common standards established by the temples of Rainvia.

Every coin bears Rainvia's scales and a ring symbolizing the road, showing that it was minted to the official standard. Strict rules for weight, metal purity, and markings ensure that coins have equal value regardless of their country of origin.

In everyday speech, people often say “one iron coin” or “three silver coins,” but formal contracts and guild requests always record amounts in Viala.

Using Viala does not require faith in Rainvia. Followers of other gods commonly use it for trade and contracts because faith in Elamnova is rooted in daily life and vocation rather than conflict between gods.

Related People and Places

Rainvia

Deity of trade, commerce, wealth, contracts, and journeys, and the source of the currency's name.

Merchants' Guild

An organization deeply involved in Viala circulation, exchange, and verification of monetary value. It connects merchants and trading companies across the world and supports commerce conducted in Viala.

Temples of Rainvia

Institutions that define Viala's monetary standards and guarantee its management and credibility. They confirm that national mints follow the standard and may witness major contracts concluded before a temple.

Money Changers

Specialists who exchange large quantities of iron, copper, and silver coins for higher denominations. They are important to merchants and adventurers who handle substantial sums.

Notes

Viala is widely used as the world's common currency. Transactions through the Adventurers' Guild and Merchants' Guild are always recorded in Viala, regardless of coin type, to standardize amounts and prevent later disputes.

Counterfeiting Viala is a serious crime. Counterfeiting gold or platinum coins in particular threatens trust between nations; guards and judicial authorities enforce the law while the temples of Rainvia assist with authentication and standards.
