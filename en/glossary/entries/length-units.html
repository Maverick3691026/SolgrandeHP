Units of Length

A shared system of length and distance used across Heiramnova, comprising Til, Riiv, Garna, and Verd.

Til, Garna, and Verd

Definition

The basic unit of length most commonly used in Heiramnova. One Riiv equals 12 Til, or approximately 31.8 cm in the real world.

Detailed Explanation

The name Riiv derives from the leaf of Lieva Grass, a plant that has grown wild since ancient times.

The central leaf of mature Lieva Grass reaches roughly from an adult fist to the elbow, and one such leaf became the original standard of length.

As trade developed, the Merchant Guild later established a standard measuring stick, and the unit spread widely as a common measure throughout Heiramnova.

Til

Conversion
12 Til = 1 Riiv / 1 Til = approximately 2.65 cm
Primary Uses
Gemstones, magic stones, coins, blade widths, seam allowances, scales, fangs, and similar small measurements

Til comes from an old merchant term meaning a division between leaf veins.

A mature Lieva Grass leaf has twelve divisions from base to tip; the length of one division came to be called one Til.

Initially used to measure small goods such as ornaments, medicinal herbs, and cloth, it eventually spread to smiths and jewelers and became a common small unit for fine dimensions.

Riiv

Conversion
1 Riiv = 12 Til / 1 Riiv = approximately 31.8 cm
Primary Uses
Human height, weapons, furniture, doors, and the body length of animals and magical beasts

Riiv derives from an old word meaning leaf.

The length of one fully mature central Lieva Grass leaf, measured from base to tip, became the prototype of one Riiv.

Because this roughly matched the distance from an adult fist to the elbow, people could estimate length with their own arm when no leaf was available.

Before trade flourished, the measure varied slightly by region, but the Merchant Guild formally standardized one Riiv. Metal and hardwood Riiv rulers were thereafter distributed to markets and workshops.

Garna

Conversion
1 Garna = 318 Riiv / 1 Garna = approximately 101.124 m
Primary Uses
Land, city walls, large buildings, city blocks, ports, bridges, and long-range distances

Garna is an old surveyors term for a measuring rope stretched taut.

When the first official measuring rope was made, two Riiv of its 320-Riiv length were used for loops and fastening knots at the ends, so its usable length of 318 Riiv became the standard.

The rope was called a Garna rope, and Garna eventually became the name of the unit representing its usable length.

Verd

Conversion
1 Verd = 318 Garna / approximately 32.157 km (precisely about 32.157432 km)
Primary Uses
Distances between cities, roads, military marches, trade routes, national territory, and sea travel

Verd was a term used by old road wardens meaning one journey or one stage from one post station to the next.

Post stations were mandated every 318 Garna along major roads, and one such interval came to be called one Verd.

Today it is used as a long-distance unit on maps, in official documents, and in requests issued by the Merchant and Adventurers Guilds, serving as a common standard across Heiramnova.

How the Unit System Developed

  1. Lieva Grass leaves are used as simple rulers.
  2. Til emerges from the leaves twelve divisions.
  3. The length of one leaf becomes established as the Riiv.
  4. Garna emerges from the Garna rope used for land surveying.
  5. Verd emerges from the distance between post stations along roads.
  6. The Merchant Guild standardizes the measures, which spread to nations and Adventurers Guilds.
