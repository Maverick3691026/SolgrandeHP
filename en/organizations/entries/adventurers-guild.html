Overview

The Adventurers Guild is an organization for adventurers found throughout the nations of Heiramnova. It serves as an intermediary for monster control, surveys of unexplored regions, private requests, and other work.

A guild headquarters is located in each national capital, while branches or local offices operate in cities and villages.

Although the guild is not a state institution, it works closely with kingdoms and cities and plays an important role in rescue and crisis response during monster incidents and natural disasters.

By registering with the guild, adventurers can accept official requests. Registration is generally open to those aged fifteen or older.

Requests cover a wide range of work, including monster subjugation, material gathering, escort duty, ruin surveys, and exploration of uncharted lands.

The guild also commonly purchases monster materials and other items acquired by adventurers during subjugation missions and expeditions.

These materials enter the market through the Merchant Guild and trading companies, where they are used to make weapons, armor, medicines, and magical tools.

During floods, earthquakes, and other disasters, adventurers may also be asked to assist with rescue and search operations.

The guild respects the freedom of adventurers and does not compel them to mobilize, even when a request comes from a nation or the guild itself.

However, cooperation in disaster relief and important missions is recorded as a Reputation rating. Adventurers with high ratings receive priority opportunities for lucrative requests, confidential missions, and national commissions.

Adventurers are assigned ranks according to ability, ranging from S rank through G rank.

This ranking system is shared across the nations of Heiramnova, and a rank earned in one country remains valid at guilds in other countries.

By connecting adventurers—whose profession is founded on freedom—with society, the Adventurers Guild has become indispensable to the safety and development of Heiramnova.

Organizational Structure

Adventurers Guild Headquarters (Capital)

The central institution of the Adventurers Guild in each national capital.

It manages ranks, issues important missions, and coordinates responses to large-scale disasters.

City Branch

A guild facility established in a major city.

It handles adventurer registration, the acceptance and brokerage of requests, reward payments, and rank evaluations.

Local Office (Village or Frontier)

A small outpost established in a small town, village, or frontier region.

It accepts simple requests, shares local monster information, and provides supply support to adventurers.

Positions

Guildmaster

The highest-ranking official in a guild.

The Guildmaster oversees operations, negotiates with nations and other organizations, and commands responses to major disasters and monster incidents.

As a rule, a headquarters Guildmaster is a former S-rank adventurer.

City-branch Guildmasters are often appointed from adventurers of A rank or higher.

Beyond individual combat ability, the role requires the overall qualities needed to lead an organization, including party command, leadership, and sound judgment.

Submaster (Deputy Guildmaster)

The officer who assists the Guildmaster.

The Submaster manages practical operations such as request administration, internal guild affairs, and paperwork, and takes command when the Guildmaster is absent.

A Submaster need not be a high-ranking adventurer; people with exceptional administrative ability, knowledge, and judgment may be appointed.

The role is often filled by a sharp-minded person skilled in magic or scholarship, or by someone accomplished in organizational management—the brains supporting guild operations.

For this reason, a Submaster may be treated more like a civilian professional whose position is unrelated to adventurer rank.

Instructor (Examiner)

The officer responsible for registration tests for new adventurers and promotion examinations.

Instructors are generally capable former adventurers and must have held at least B rank.

This is a safety measure: requests of C rank and above increasingly involve dangerous work such as monster subjugation, so unqualified adventurers must not be sent recklessly into danger.

Consequently, C rank is considered the first major hurdle for many adventurers and is the rank with the largest population.

Receptionist

A staff member responsible for accepting requests, paying rewards, and managing request boards.

Receptionists serve as the point of contact connecting adventurers and clients and play an important role in guild operations.

They are not adventurers but employees hired by the guild.

Many begin as apprentices and must accurately understand guild regulations, the request system, and reward structures.

Because purchasing monster materials, dropped goods, and other items brought back by adventurers is also guild business, receptionists need the knowledge to understand material values and market prices and to conduct fair appraisals.

The intermediary nature of the job therefore requires impartial judgment, courteous service, friendliness, and a working knowledge of materials and market prices.

The demanding skill set makes receptionist work a comparatively well-compensated clerical profession.

Related Organizations

Government of the Host Nation (and Allied Governments)

The Adventurers Guild is not a state institution, but it maintains close cooperation with the government of its host nation.

In emergencies such as monster incidents and natural disasters, the government may request assistance with rescue or subjugation.

Missions may also be conducted in cooperation with allied guilds and governments under international alliances.

Merchant Guild

An organization closely connected through requests for caravan escorts and surveys of dangerous regions.

Much of the monster material and rare resources brought back by adventurers reaches the market through the Merchant Guild and trading companies.

Trading Companies

Commercial organizations that conduct long-distance trade and operate caravans.

They frequently submit requests to the Adventurers Guild for caravan escorts and exploration of dangerous regions.
