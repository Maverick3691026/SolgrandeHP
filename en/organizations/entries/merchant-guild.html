Overview

The Merchant Guild is a commercial organization found throughout the nations of Heiramnova and an economic association whose members include merchants, traders, artisans, and distributors.

A Merchant Guild headquarters is located in each national capital, with branches in major cities.

In cities and trading hubs, it manages markets and mediates transactions, maintaining order in commercial activity.

By registering with the guild, merchants can obtain official trade permits, rights to use markets, and brokerage for escort requests.

The guild stabilizes prices, arbitrates transactions, and manages distribution, making it a central institution in urban economies.

Monster materials and rare resources brought back by adventurers are purchased through the Adventurers Guild, and much of this merchandise enters the market through the Merchant Guild and trading companies.

Materials and items can also be sold directly to shops in town, but only when they fall within the categories handled by a given shop.

Because the Adventurers Guild can purchase many different materials in a single transaction, shops buy such goods comparatively infrequently.

Caravans engaged in long-distance trade often submit escort requests to the Adventurers Guild through the Merchant Guild, creating a close relationship between the two organizations.

To maintain major trade routes and ensure market safety, the Merchant Guild also cooperates with national governments and plays an important role in the economic development of Heiramnova.

Numerous individual merchants and trading companies are registered with the Merchant Guild, which supports and manages their commercial activity.

Organizational Structure

Merchant Guild Headquarters (Capital)

The central institution of the Merchant Guild in each national capital.

It negotiates with the state, coordinates trade policy, manages large caravans, and regulates major markets.

City Branch

A guild institution established in a major city.

It supports everyday commercial activity through merchant registration, market administration, issuance of trade permits, and transaction arbitration.

Local Office (Village or Trading Hub)

An outpost established in a small town, village, or roadside trading hub.

It manages caravan rest stops, supplies provisions, supports simple transactions, and shares information.

Related Organizations

Adventurers Guild

The Merchant Guild works closely with the Adventurers Guild, which brokers requests for caravan escorts, surveys of dangerous regions, and monster subjugation.

Government of the Host Nation (and Allied Governments)

The Merchant Guild cooperates with its host government on maintaining trade routes, ensuring market safety, and shaping economic policy.

Trade may also be conducted jointly with merchant guilds and governments of allied nations under international alliances.

Because goods or information could otherwise flow to enemy nations, the Merchant Guild generally prohibits trade with countries that lack formal diplomatic relations, such as alliances or friendly ties.

Trading Companies

Commercial organizations registered with the Merchant Guild.

They conduct long-distance trade and operate caravans, forming the core of the guild economic activity.
