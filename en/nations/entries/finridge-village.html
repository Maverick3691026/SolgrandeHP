Finridge Village

A small village in the Principality of Bermouthia that developed through the cultivation and gathering of medicinal herbs.

Laws

Customs and Traditions

Finridge Village follows the laws of the Principality of Bermouthia. Because it developed through herb cultivation and research, the villagers also observe customs formed over generations rather than formal laws.

Rules for Gathering Herbs

The surrounding forests and grasslands are important habitats for medicinal plants. Gathered herbs are customarily reported to the village herbalist or herb keeper, and rare plants may not be removed without permission. Visitors and adventurers are expected to seek guidance before gathering.

Respect for the Forest

Wasteful logging and excessive hunting are discouraged. To prevent forest fires, open flames and the use of fire-element magic in combat are forbidden within the forest.

Travelers

Alchemists and herbalists sometimes visit the remote village. Most stay at its small tavern and inn, but anyone gathering or researching in the forest is expected to consult the villagers first.

Resolving Disputes

Minor disputes are usually settled through mediation by the village chief or elders. Murder and other serious crimes are referred to the courts of Tsadar.

Commemorating the Village's Founding

A stone monument honors the pioneer Hiram Grün. The exact founding date is unknown, but a small annual Hiram Festival is held around the traditional season, when villagers share herbs and food in gratitude for the land and their predecessors.

Politics

Finridge is a small self-governing village under Tsadar. The village chief handles daily administration, while elders and influential residents discuss important matters. Taxation and major trials remain under Tsadar's authorities.

Military

The village has no standing army. A small volunteer watch handles dangerous animals, monsters, night patrols, and local security. Retired adventurers living in the village provide much of its combat experience, while full military defense is the responsibility of Tsadar's garrison.

Religion

Local belief is rooted in the nature worship found throughout Bermouthia. Villagers quietly honor the blessings of the forest, earth, and medicinal herbs. The village's small old church serves as a place of prayer, education, and community meetings, and is also home to a priest born in Finridge.

The pioneer Hiram Grün is remembered with deep respect rather than worship. His monument and the annual festival preserve the memory of his achievement.

Language

The Common Tongue is the primary language. It is widely used throughout Elamnova in trade, administration, scholarship, and many other settings. Everyday speech retains a small number of expressions and dialect features particular to the Principality of Bermouthia.

Economy

The main industries are cultivating and gathering medicinal herbs. Finridge herbs are valued for their quality and are carried mainly to Tsadar and other settlements in Bermouthia, where alchemists and herbalists use them in medicines and potions.

Villagers also practice subsistence farming and hunting. Visiting merchants occasionally trade household goods for local herbs.

Education

Finridge has no formal school. Children learn reading, writing, common knowledge, and morals from the priest at the church three mornings a week, when few worshippers are present. They also learn the effects, handling, and simple preparation of herbs by helping their families from an early age.

Food Culture

Finridge cuisine makes generous use of medicinal and culinary herbs. Herb-roasted game is a village staple, while herb-infused liquor is served at the tavern. Travelers often regard both as essential tastes of Finridge, though villagers know them as ordinary home fare.

Population

Finridge has 167 residents. Most are humans engaged in herb cultivation and gathering. Humans are the most populous race in Elamnova and also make up most of Finridge's population. A small number of beastfolk and other demi-human residents have also been drawn to the village's natural environment.

Public Safety

Strong community ties make serious crime rare. Minor problems are usually resolved through discussion or mediation. The village watch patrols at night and deals with wild animals and low-level monsters from the surrounding forest.

Main Facilities

Church

The small old church is used for prayer, lessons, village meetings, and as the residence of the local priest, Cassian Hales, who also advises villagers on everyday concerns.

Smithy and Village Chief's Home

The home of village chief Ruben includes a smithy that produces farming tools, household implements, and weapons for the village watch.

Clinic

Beside the smithy is a small clinic run by Ruben's wife, Céline, who uses her knowledge of herbs to treat patients and prepare potions.

General Store

The shop run by their son Darius sells locally made weapons, tools, cookware, potions, medicines, and daily necessities.

Hearthroot Tavern and Inn

The village's only tavern and inn is run by the widowed proprietress Marta and her daughter Emma. The first floor has about twenty seats, while the second has six guest rooms. Its specialties are herb-infused liquor and herb-roasted game.

Hiram's Monument

A stone monument at the center of the village honors pioneer Hiram Grün and symbolizes the village's history.
