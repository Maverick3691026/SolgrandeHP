Principality of Bermouthia — National Overview

A neutral principality ruled by a grand duke. It treats magic, alchemy, and pharmacy as national assets and forms one part of the southern defense zone.

Laws

Bermouthia strictly regulates social status, human trafficking, magical technology, natural resources, diplomacy, and military affairs in order to preserve public order and safety.

Offenses are broadly divided into minor crimes, serious crimes, and high treason. Minor or first offenses may result in fines, confiscation, suspension of business, or penitential labor. Kidnapping, murder, slave trading, and major leaks of protected technology can lead to long imprisonment, life sentences, or severe physical labor. Rebellion, disclosure of military intelligence, and aid to hostile forces are treated as high treason and may be punished by execution.

Penitential Labor

  • Forest maintenance
  • Assistance in medicinal-herb fields
  • Road maintenance
  • Restoration of areas damaged by monsters

1. Human Rights and Social Status

Slavery and slave trading are prohibited. Buying or selling people, forced confinement and labor, kidnapping, and murder are serious crimes. Rebellion against the principality is punishable as a grave threat to national order.

2. Magic, Alchemy, and Pharmacy

Knowledge and technology in these fields are protected national assets. Unauthorized transfer abroad and the unlicensed sale of magical tools, alchemical materials, or medicines are punished according to severity.

Hazardous Materials

  • Unauthorized manufacture of dangerous medicines is prohibited
  • Explosive alchemical materials are restricted
  • Cursed tools, poisons, and prohibited medicines may not be traded
  • Dangerous experiments outside academies are prohibited

3. Nature Conservation and Land Use

Logging and land development require state permission. Harvesting that exhausts wild herb colonies is prohibited, while local leaders set gathering seasons, quantities, and hunting ranges according to regional conditions.

4. Diplomacy and Trade

Although neutral, Bermouthia has a defense pact with the Mithril Throne of Kazandor and the Fortress Duchy of Tarrenfall. Together they form the southern defense zone. Trade with those two states is broader than with nations outside the pact, while magical, alchemical, pharmaceutical, and military goods and information remain under strict control.

  • The south toward Tarrenfall is treated as a joint defense zone
  • Neutrality is maintained toward the north, east, and west
  • Export of military, magical, and alchemical technology is strictly restricted

5. Military and Defense Law

Disclosure of military secrets is high treason. Bermouthia's fertile land and abundant resources have repeatedly made it a target, leading to a strong defensive system. Mountain terrain, Tarrenfall's fortress defenses, magical forces, and golems combine into a layered southern defense.

Politics

Bermouthia is centrally governed by its grand duke, supported by the ducal administration, public agencies, regional leaders, the Adventurers' Guild, and the Merchants' Guild. The grand duke decides major matters of diplomacy, military affairs, law, and national policy, while officials handle daily administration.

1. The Grand Duke and Central Government

The current ruler is Leonis Bermouthia XII. He holds final authority over the defense pact, the southern defense zone, trade restrictions, and military intelligence. Tsadar is the political, economic, and academic center.

2. Ducal and Administrative Institutions

Government institutions enforce laws, manage cities and trade permits, regulate land development and forests, and oversee magic, alchemy, and pharmacy.

3. Cooperation with the Adventurers' Guild

The guild is independent but works closely with the government on monsters, unexplored regions, material gathering, escorts, and disasters. Cooperation is voluntary and contributes to an adventurer's trust rating.

4. Cooperation with the Merchants' Guild

The guild manages markets, arbitrates transactions, maintains distribution and trade routes, and helps regulate protected goods. It prioritizes stable logistics with Kazandor and Tarrenfall and coordinates escorts when necessary.

5. Regional Government

Local leaders regulate land use, herb gathering, and hunting, while actions affecting the principality as a whole remain under central authorization. Major developments and local conditions are reported to Tsadar.

Military

Bermouthia does not wage wars of conquest. Its military doctrine prioritizes defending cities, farmland, resource zones, and trade routes. The southern mountains form a natural barrier whose sole passage is guarded by Tarrenfall.

1. Golem Forces

The principality's defining military strength is its earth-magic golem force. Because one person cannot control hundreds of golems, graduates of Tsadar's magic academy with strong earth affinity are recruited voluntarily and offered stable, favorable positions.

2. Magic Corps

Elemental mages form the army's core. Earth magic is especially valued for walls, terrain control, fortification, and golems; fire supports interception, water supports firefighting and supply, wind supports communication and reconnaissance, and derived elements serve specialized missions.

3. Knights and Standing Army

Graduates trained in swordsmanship, spears, shields, horsemanship, and command serve in the regular army. Knights support magical and golem units through city security, gate defense, escort duty, and maintenance of public order.

4. Defensive Doctrine

The doctrine is not an army for attack, but an army that prevents invasion. Tarrenfall blocks the invasion route, Kazandor supplies high-quality arms and fortress materials, and Bermouthia provides magic, golems, medicines, and resources.

5. Military Characteristics

  • A neutral nation specialized in defense
  • Magic corps and golems are its main forces
  • Earth magic is central to defensive doctrine
  • Tsadar's magic academy supports military talent
  • Knights serve as defensive support for magical forces
  • Cooperation with Kazandor and Tarrenfall forms the southern defense zone

Social Classes

Bermouthian society includes the poor, commoners, influential commoners and senior specialists, nobles, and the grand ducal house. Slavery and slave trading are prohibited, and no legal class treats people as property.
