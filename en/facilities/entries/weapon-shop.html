Weapon Shop

A commercial establishment specializing in the sale and purchase of weapons.

Overview

A commercial establishment specializing in the sale and purchase of weapons.

It carries a broad range, from common weapons such as swords, spears, and bows to shields, throwing weapons, and backup arms. Stock varies by region: urban shops offer high-quality weapons and rare equipment, while frontier shops focus on practical arms.

Structure and Equipment

Display shelves and stands are arranged inside the shop, organized by purpose and type.

Large stores may include a practice area for testing swings and a consultation counter, while valuable weapons and custom orders are kept under strict control in storage rooms.

Role

Besides supplying adventurers and soldiers with weapons, shops inspect weapon condition and arrange suitable maintenance or referrals to smithies.

Demand changes with local monster activity and military conditions, so weapon shops also play an important role in supporting a town's defense.

Operation and Management

Most are independently or family operated, and many belong to the Merchants' Guild.

Shops partnered with smithies also accept custom-production and repair requests. Rare weapons may be offered first to trusted customers.

History

After an era in which smiths sold the weapons they made themselves, increased weapon circulation led weapon shops to emerge as independent specialty stores.

Today they cooperate with smithies and merchants' guilds across the land, serving as key distribution centers that supply weapons throughout Heiramnova.

Notes

The types and quality of weapons carried vary by region and nation.

Rare and magical weapons may be handled only by specialty shops or stores operating by referral.

Some shops also appraise weapons and broker custom-production orders.
