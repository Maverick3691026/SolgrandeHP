Fluffer

Fluffer

A low-threat herbivorous beast monster found widely across grasslands and forests.

Basic Information

Name
Fluffer
Classification
Beast-type monster
Threat Level
Low
Habitat
Forests and grasslands
Drops
Fur, meat, and Fluffer magic stone

Overview

Fluffers are herbivorous beast-type monsters found widely in grasslands and forests throughout the world.

Their high fertility and enormous population make them one of the world's most familiar monsters. Their combat ability is low, so the Adventurers' Guild often uses them for novice training and beginner quests.

Ecology

Fluffers are diurnal herbivores that live in groups. They feed on weeds, young leaves, and nuts, consuming several tens of percent of their own body weight in vegetation each day.

They reproduce several times a year, keeping their numbers stable throughout the world. Wolves and large carnivorous monsters are among their many natural predators and keep their population in balance.

When danger is detected, the entire group flees at once while sounding a warning cry. Only when escape seems impossible will a Fluffer defend itself by spitting “Fluffer gel.”

Appearance

Fluffers have round black eyes, long ears, and pure-white double-coated fur covering their entire bodies. No brown, black, or otherwise colored individuals have been confirmed.

Temperament

They are extremely timid and wary, fleeing quickly when they sense people or large monsters.

Characteristics

Powerful hind legs give them excellent jumping ability, agility, and exceptional speed when fleeing.

They normally avoid combat, but when cornered they spit a slime called Fluffer gel. It produces an overpowering stench that remains on clothing or equipment for roughly ten days.

This is their only and greatest defense, so experienced adventurers aim to kill them before they can spit. Though the monsters themselves pose little danger, the lingering smell may leave equipment unusable for some time; adventurers therefore call them “novice tormentors, not novice killers.”

Drops

Fur, meat, and Fluffer magic stones.

The insulating fur is used for winter clothing and pet bedding. The leg meat is high in protein and low in fat, making it popular among those engaged in strength training. Though it appears tough and muscular, cooking gives it a pleasant balance of tenderness and chew with a mild flavor.

“Fluffer skewers” are a widely loved specialty in the rural Bermouthian town of Aigelton.

Fluffer magic stones are purchased by guilds and merchants as materials for magical tools. Their vast supply keeps their market value low, so they provide little income.

Notes

Fluffers help maintain grassland environments by eating weeds, but they also consume vegetables and grain in fields, causing farmers to describe them with a rueful smile as “helpful nuisances.”

Researchers have studied the foul-smelling Fluffer gel as a repellent to protect farms and livestock from low-rank monsters. Its persistent odor and strong effect on humans have so far prevented practical use.
