Owlbear

Owlbear

An intelligent magical beast with a powerfully built body.

Threat Level

An ordinary adult is considered approximately C-rank, but a superior individual that has lived for many years, accumulated experience, and acquired advanced intelligence and magical skill poses a B-rank threat.

Habitat

Deep forests and mountainous regions.

Overview

Owlbears are intelligent magical beasts with powerfully built bodies. They excel at attacking with the formidable claws on their well-developed limbs.

Though they cannot fly because they lack wings, they can run remarkably fast despite their massive bodies, reaching speeds of approximately 2.5 Verd per hour.

They are extremely wary, making wild individuals difficult to tame.

To raise one in human care, training must begin in infancy and continue over a long period.

Even ordinary individuals are intelligent enough to understand the common language to some degree, but only superior individuals that have lived for many years and accumulated experience can speak it themselves. They do not acquire the common language naturally; they need an environment in which they can learn it, such as being taught by a human trainer or by another individual that has already learned it.

Ecology

Owlbears inhabit deep forests and mountainous regions, preferring caves, abandoned mines, and similar places as their dens.

A male and female form a pair and remain together for life. Both parents cooperate in raising their young.

They are highly intelligent. Even ordinary individuals can understand the common language to some extent, while some long-lived and experienced individuals can speak it.

They are omnivorous and will eat nuts and weeds when food is scarce, but they especially favor the meat of magical beasts such as boars and Fluffers.

Appearance

Owlbears are magical beasts with an owl-like head and the enormous body of a bear.

The head bears a sharp, well-developed beak, while large, sharp claws grow from the ends of their thick and powerful limbs.

Characteristics

They can use lightning magic and discharge beginner-level lightning spells from their mouths.

Magical proficiency varies between individuals, and one individual capable of using Thunder Whip has been recorded in the past.

They may also emit an overwhelming aura when showing hostility. The intimidation of a particularly experienced superior individual can be so intense that an unseasoned person freezes and finds it difficult even to move.

With proper training from infancy, an Owlbear can be raised as a magical beast capable of carrying riders and serving as a guard.

Young Owlbears therefore command high prices. On extremely rare occasions, individuals with entirely white fur, known as albinos, are found.

Albino individuals have higher overall abilities than ordinary Owlbears. Young albinos in particular sell for prices far exceeding those of ordinary young because of their rarity and usefulness after training.

Individuals raising young are even more wary than usual and become extremely aggressive toward anyone they believe may endanger their offspring.

Drops

Owlbear claws

Their exceptional sharpness and hardness make them useful as materials for crafting weapons.

Owlbear fur

The coarse, rigid fur is used as a material for armor.

Owlbear meat

Although highly nutritious, its extremely strong odor makes it unsuitable for eating. It is mainly processed into fertilizer.

Owlbear magic stone

A magic stone with lightning properties. When incorporated into armor or accessories, it grants resistance to lightning.

Notes

Because Owlbears remain with the same mate for life, people cherish them as symbols of harmonious relationships.

Accessories crafted from Owlbear claws are therefore popular among couples and spouses.
